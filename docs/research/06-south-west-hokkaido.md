# 06 — Kansai, Setouchi, Kyushu, Hokkaido: experience catalog for 20 Dec 2026 – 9 Jan 2027

Researched 1 Oct 2026. Group of six: planner + partner at 29–32 weeks (no long stair climbs, ≤ ~3 h transit on moving days, rest built in), brother + sister-in-law (30s, active), two very fit parents. In/out of Tokyo; a domestic flight back to Haneda from a far region is allowed.

Conventions used in cards: **bump_ok** 1 (avoid) – 5 (ideal); **effort** 1 (flat, short) – 5 (long/steep); **NY status** = what the thing is doing 29 Dec – 3 Jan. Costs are per adult in JPY unless noted. "Confirm" = official dates for 2026-27 not yet published at research time; the pattern is from the previous one or two editions.

Cross-region facts that shape everything:

- **Nozomi all-reserved window.** JR Central/West make every Nozomi seat reserved-only during the New Year peak (26 Dec – 4 Jan in 2025-26; expect the same shape for 2026-27). Reservations open 1 month ahead at 10:00 JST; six seats together on 28–30 Dec and 2–4 Jan need to be booked the day they open (SmartEX or JR West online). (smart-ex.jp)
- **What closes.** Museums and castles generally close 29 Dec – 1 Jan (Himeji 29–30 Dec; Hiroshima Peace Museum 30–31 Dec; Okayama Castle 29–31 Dec; Ohara Museum 28–31 Dec; Benesse sites partly). Shrines, temples, gardens, onsen towns, ryokan, department-store food halls (from 2 Jan) and theme parks stay open. Kuromon/Nishiki market stalls peak 29–31 Dec, then many shut 1–3 Jan.
- **Hatsumode (1–3 Jan)** is the one time a year Japanese visit shrines en masse: Fushimi Inari ~2.7–2.8 M, Dazaifu > 2 M, Sumiyoshi Taisha ~2 M. Treat it as a spectacle to opt into deliberately (go 1 Jan early morning or at midnight, or 2–3 Jan after 15:00), never as a drop-in.
- **Pregnancy and onsen.** Japan's Environment Ministry removed pregnancy from the general onsen contraindication list in 2014; ordinary soaking in moderate water (≤ 40 °C, ≤ 10–15 min, hydrated) is widely accepted. The specific no-gos are **Ibusuki sand baths** (facilities explicitly refuse pregnant guests), saunas, very hot (42 °C+) and acidic/sulphur-heavy baths, and slippery outdoor rotenburo in snow. Private family baths (kashikiri) solve the "six people, mixed sexes, one pregnant" problem everywhere.

## 1. Kansai (Kyoto · Nara · Osaka · Kōyasan · Kinosaki · Arima · Himeji · Kobe)

### Why these dates

- **Kyoto New Year is the real thing.** Ōmisoka (31 Dec) brings Chion-in's 70-ton bell (17 monks, gates open ~20:30, bell ~22:40–00:00, observation only; people queue from ~20:00 in the cold) and Yasaka Shrine's Okera-mairi (sacred fire from ~19:00 31 Dec to 05:00 1 Jan, free; buy a rope, light it, spin it home). 1–3 Jan is hatsumode; 2.7 M+ at Fushimi Inari. Nothing else in Japan beats Kyoto for this week. (iwafu.com, kyoto-np.com)
- **Food.** Matsuba crab season on the Sea of Japan coast runs 7 Nov – 31 Mar; Kinosaki ryokan crab kaiseki is at its best in late Dec/Jan. Osechi and ozōni in Kyoto; fugu in Osaka; Kobe beef any day except 1 Jan when many restaurants close. (japanactivity.com)
- **Weather.** Kyoto/Osaka/Nara 2–10 °C, mostly dry; Kōyasan and Kinosaki 0 to -5 °C with snow possible; Kinosaki coast is grey and wet. Short days (sunset ~17:00).
- **Festivals in window.** Tōka Ebisu at Imamiya Ebisu, Osaka, **9–11 Jan**, > 1 M visitors (fits the last day of the trip only if the group flies out of Tokyo late on 9 Jan or has already moved). Kobe Luminarie is **29 Jan – 7 Feb 2027**, so it is **outside** the window. USJ runs a 26-hour Countdown (31 Dec 19:00 – 1 Jan 21:00, Party Pass from ¥16,900). (japancheapo.com, usj.co.jp, watabi.org)

### Experience cards

**KS-01 · Sino de Ano Novo no Chion-in / Chion-in Joya-no-Kane (31 Dec)**
17 monks swing a log into Japan's largest temple bell 108 times; viewing only, free. Gates ~20:30, bell from ~22:40. Three hours standing in 2–4 °C.
Suits: parents, brother/SIL. Bump: 2 (standing, crowds, cold; partner could do Okera-mairi instead and meet after). Effort 3. Outdoor. Free. 3–4 h. No booking. NY: this *is* NY. Uniqueness 5. (iwafu.com, mykyotomachiya.com)

**KS-02 · Okera-mairi no Santuário Yasaka / Yasaka Shrine Okera-mairi (31 Dec night)**
Light a rope from the sacred fire and spin it home through Gion. Flat, 10 min from any Gion machiya, go 18:00–20:00 before the crush. Combines with a late kaiseki.
Suits: all six. Bump 4 (flat, can leave anytime). Effort 1. Outdoor. Free (+ ¥500 rope). 1 h. No booking. NY: 31 Dec only. Uniqueness 5. (kyoto-np.com, japaninsides.com)

**KS-03 · Hatsumōde em Fushimi Inari / Fushimi Inari at New Year**
Torii tunnels are open 24 h and lit; at midnight 1 Jan or 06:00–08:00 the lower loop is doable; the full 4 km summit loop is for the fit four. 2–3 Jan after 15:00 cuts queues to 30–60 min.
Suits: split — lower loop for all, summit for parents/brother. Bump 2 at midnight (crush), 4 at dawn. Effort 2 (lower) / 4 (summit). Outdoor. Free. 1.5–3 h. No booking. NY: peak. Uniqueness 4. (japanactivity.com)

**KS-04 · Mercado Nishiki na véspera / Nishiki Market year-end rush (29–31 Dec)**
Kyoto buys its osechi here; wall-to-wall by 11:00. Go at 09:00 on 30 Dec, graze, leave. Most stalls shut 1–3 Jan.
Suits: all, in two trios. Bump 3 (crowds, but flat and short). Effort 1. Covered. ¥2,000–4,000 grazing. 1.5 h. No booking. NY: open 29–31, mostly closed 1–3 Jan. Uniqueness 3.

**KS-05 · Uji: Byōdō-in e matcha / Uji Byōdō-in and tea street (+ Nintendo Museum)**
15 min by JR from Kyoto. Phoenix Hall (open 1 Jan, ¥700), flat riverside, matcha everything. Nintendo Museum is 1 km from Ogura station and is lottery-only: apply in the single window ~3 months out (Jan 2027 draw closed 31 Oct 2026; **Dec 2026 and early-Jan applications are already past or closing**; check the calendar for a resale/on-sale slot). Museum closed 30 Dec – 2 Jan typically.
Suits: all; Nintendo for brother/SIL if they win the draw. Bump 5. Effort 1. Mixed. ¥700 + ¥3,300 museum. Half day. Lottery, 3 months. NY: Byōdō-in open; museum confirm. Uniqueness 3 (Uji) / 4 (Nintendo). (museum-tickets.nintendo.com, japanticketradar.com)

**KS-06 · Ōhara no inverno: Sanzen-in e Jakkō-in / Ōhara in winter**
Bus 17 from Kyoto station, 60 min. Moss garden with a dusting of snow 1–3 times a winter, 9:00–16:30 in Dec–Feb. Sanzen-in has ~60 steps on the approach; Jakkō-in is a 20-min flat country walk then a short stair.
Suits: all; parents can add the Jakkō-in walk. Bump 3 (bus is 60 min each way; steps manageable). Effort 2. Outdoor. ¥700 + ¥600. Half day. No booking. NY: open, quiet 1–3 Jan. Uniqueness 4. (japan-experience.com, kanpai-japan.com)

**KS-07 · Kurama–Kibune e Kurama Onsen / Kurama–Kibune walk + Kurama Onsen**
Eizan line 30 min. The temple-to-Kibune ridge walk (2.5 km, 1.5 h, steep steps and tree roots, icy in Jan) is a parents/brother outing; Kurama Onsen (rebuilt, reopened Nov 2024) has an outdoor bath for a post-walk soak. Partner stays at Kibune's riverside or skips.
Suits: fit four. Bump 1 (walk) / 3 (onsen only). Effort 4. Outdoor. ¥500 temple + ¥1,100–2,500 onsen. Half day. No booking. NY: temple open; onsen confirm. Uniqueness 3. (japan-guide.com, kurama-spa.com)

**KS-08 · Tōdai-ji à meia-noite / Tōdai-ji New Year opening (0:00–8:00, 1 Jan)**
Chūmon gate opens at midnight; free entry to the Daibutsuden and the hall's upper window is opened so the Buddha's lit face is seen from the courtyard — once a year besides Obon. Bell tickets (108 strikes, groups of 6–8 per rope) are handed out ~22:30 31 Dec. Flat park, deer awake. Then 06:00–08:00 is the calm window.
Suits: all; pick midnight (atmosphere) or 07:00 (calm). Bump 3 at midnight (cold, 45 min from Kyoto by Kintetsu, last trains run all night on 31 Dec), 5 at dawn. Effort 1. Outdoor/indoor. Free. 2 h. No booking (bell: ticket at 22:30). NY: unique to 1 Jan. Uniqueness 5. (x.com/todaiji, blogs.transparent.com)

**KS-09 · Cervos de Nara e Kasuga Taisha / Nara Park deer + Kasuga Taisha lantern paths**
Flat, 24 h, free; Kasuga Taisha 07:00–17:00 in winter (¥700 inner), 2 Jan 10:00 and 3 Jan 11:00 rituals. Deer crackers ¥200. Combine with KS-08 or a separate day.
Suits: all. Bump 5. Effort 1. Outdoor. ¥700. 3 h. No booking. NY: open. Uniqueness 3. (narashikanko.or.jp)

**KS-10 · Yoshino no inverno / Mt Yoshino in winter (Kinpusen-ji Zaō-dō)**
Nearly empty; Zaō-dō (national treasure) and ridge views. Ropeway runs only Fri–Mon off-season, bus Tue–Thu. 1.5 h each way from Kyoto via Kintetsu. Hilly village street.
Suits: parents/brother as a day out; weak value for the partner. Bump 2. Effort 3. Outdoor. ¥800 + ropeway ¥450. Full day. No booking. NY: temple open; ropeway confirm. Uniqueness 2 in winter. Honest view: cut unless someone is a Shugendō fan. (visitnara.jp)

**KS-11 · Kuromon e Dōtonbori na virada / Kuromon Market + Dōtonbori (29–31 Dec)**
Kuromon has extended hours 29–30 Dec (crab, fugu, uni), most stalls shut 1–4 Jan. Dōtonbori is open through NY and heaving on 31 Dec (no official countdown; crowds gather at the Glico sign). Flat, 10 min apart.
Suits: all. Bump 3 (crowds; sit-down kushikatsu or kaiten places let the partner rest). Effort 1. Mixed. ¥3,000–6,000. 3 h. No booking. NY: Kuromon 29–31 only; Dōtonbori open. Uniqueness 3. (tripadvisor.com, kuromon.com)

**KS-12 · Tōka Ebisu em Imamiya Ebisu / Tōka Ebisu (9–11 Jan)**
Osaka's merchants' festival: fukuzasa bamboo, 500-person palanquin parade on 10 Jan, > 1 M visitors over 3 days. 9 Jan is "Yoi Ebisu" — the only day inside the trip, and only if the flight home is late on 9 Jan from KIX/ITM. Otherwise cut.
Suits: all if timing works. Bump 2 (dense crowd). Effort 2. Outdoor. Free. 2 h. No booking. NY: 9–11 Jan. Uniqueness 4. (japancheapo.com, osakatourism.org)

**KS-13 · Cachoeira de Minoo e tempurá de momiji / Minoo Falls walk**
2.7 km paved, nearly flat path from Hankyu Minoo station to a 33 m waterfall; momiji tempura stalls open year-round, quieter in winter. Good "rest day" outing from Osaka.
Suits: all. Bump 5. Effort 2. Outdoor. Free + ¥500 snacks. 2.5 h. No booking. NY: open. Uniqueness 2. (jeepe.jp)

**KS-14 · USJ Countdown 2027 / Universal Studios Japan NO LIMIT! Countdown (31 Dec–1 Jan)**
26-hour park (19:00 31 Dec – 21:00 1 Jan), Super Nintendo World unlimited, countdown at 23:50. Party Pass from ¥16,900, Lawson pre-sale then general sale from Oct. Pregnant guests are barred from most coasters/Mario Kart; the partner would be there for atmosphere only.
Suits: brother/SIL (split night while others do Kyoto bells). Bump 1. Effort 3. Mixed. ¥16,900+. Overnight. Booking: buy at general sale (Oct–Nov). NY: the event. Uniqueness 3. (usj.co.jp, tdrexplorer.com)

**KS-15 · Noite em templo em Kōyasan + Okunoin à noite / Kōyasan shukubō + Okunoin night walk**
From Namba: Nankai express ~1.5 h to Gokurakubashi + cable car 5 min + bus 10 min (cable car and line operating normally as of Oct 2026; snow is normal in Jan, trains run). Temple lodging (Eko-in, Fukuchi-in, Sōji-in) with shōjin-ryōri and 06:00 fire ritual; Eko-in's English Okunoin night tour leaves 19:00 nightly year-round, ~90 min, ~¥2,500 on site (reseller listings up to ¥6,000). Okunoin is 2 km flat gravel; the main temples have 10–40 steps. Rooms are cold and shared toilets are common — pick a shukubō with ensuite (Fukuchi-in, Sōji-in) for the partner. Shukubō stay open through NY and host their own bell ritual on 31 Dec; book 3–4 months ahead for 30 Dec–2 Jan.
Suits: all. Bump 3 (one-night stay, flat Okunoin; cold). Effort 2. Mixed. ¥18,000–30,000 pp incl. 2 meals + ¥2,500 tour. 1 night. Booking 3–4 months. NY: open. Uniqueness 5. (traffic.nankai.co.jp, sites.google.com/view/ekoin-en, onedayawaytravel.com)

**KS-16 · Kinosaki: caranguejo e os 7 banhos / Kinosaki Onsen crab + sotoyu circuit**
Flat willow-lined town, yukata and geta from the ryokan, 7 public baths on a free pass — **only 5 open in 2026** (Satono-yu closed since Apr 2024 for rebuild; Kō-no-yu closed to 30 Oct 2026, should be back by Dec). Crab kaiseki (full Matsuba crab ¥35,000–60,000 pp at top inns; "half crab" plans ¥25,000). Nishimuraya Honkan has several 2–6-person suites; Kinosaki also has 3–4 rooms per inn for a six-split. New Year's at Kinosaki is sold out 3–6 months ahead; call/email the inn directly in Sept–Oct. Train: Kyoto → Kinosaki Kinosaki Express ~2 h 30, no transfer; Osaka ~2 h 45. The town itself is the most bump-friendly onsen base in Japan (no hills, short walks, ryokan baths at 40–41 °C).
Suits: all. Bump 5. Effort 1. Mixed. ¥30,000–60,000 pp per night. 1–2 nights. Booking 3–6 months. NY: open, premium rates. Uniqueness 5. (hotspringstays.com, japanactivity.com, nishimuraya.ne.jp)

**KS-17 · Castelo de Himeji / Himeji Castle**
Closed 29–30 Dec, open 1 Jan. Inside the keep is six storeys of steep wooden stairs — the partner stops at the grounds and Kōko-en garden (flat, ¥360 combo). 40 min by Shinkansen from Kyoto/Osaka; combine with Kobe or Okayama transit.
Suits: fit four inside; partner garden only. Bump 3 (grounds) / 1 (keep). Effort 4 keep. Outdoor/indoor. ¥1,000. 2.5 h. No booking (timed entry at peak). NY: closed 29–30 Dec. Uniqueness 4. (visit-himeji.com)

**KS-18 · Arima Onsen: Kin-no-yu e Gin-no-yu / Arima Onsen gold & silver baths**
Oldest onsen in Japan, 30 min from Kobe by bus/Rokkō cable. Both public baths **closed 1 Jan**; Kin-no-yu is iron-rich 42–44 °C (too hot for the partner; Gin-no-yu's carbonated bath at ~40 °C is better). Hilly lanes. Half-day or one night (Nakanobō Zuien, Gekkōen have 6-person rooms).
Suits: all except on 1 Jan. Bump 3. Effort 2. Indoor. ¥800 / ¥700. Half day. No booking (ryokan: 3 months). NY: open except 1 Jan. Uniqueness 3. (gekkoen.co.jp, jeepe.jp)

**KS-19 · Carne de Kobe em Sannomiya / Kobe beef dinner**
Teppanyaki counters (Ishida, Mouriya, Kobe Plaisir) ~¥12,000–25,000 pp; most close 31 Dec–2 Jan. Flat Sannomiya. Luminarie is **not** in window (29 Jan–7 Feb 2027).
Suits: all. Bump 5. Effort 1. Indoor. ¥15,000. 2 h. Book 2–4 weeks (6 seats at a counter). NY: closed 1–2 Jan at most. Uniqueness 3. (japancheapo.com)

### Bases for six and lodging outlook

- **Kyoto, Higashiyama / Gion / Shimogyō:** 2–3-bedroom machiya rentals (Machiya Residence Inn, My Kyoto Machiya, Kyoto Machiya Inn, Airbnb) sleep 5–7; expect ¥60,000–120,000/night over NY and **some operators block 25 Dec – 8 Jan** or require 3+ nights. Alternatives: two adjacent 3-person rooms in a hotel with a lift (Mitsui Garden, Hotel The Celestine Gion) — easier for the partner than a machiya's steep interior stair (ask for a ground-floor bedroom). Book by mid-Oct 2026. (kyoto-machiya-inn.com, wabunka-lux.jp)
- **Osaka, Namba/Shinsaibashi:** apartment hotels (Mimaru, Fraser, Oakwood) with 2-bed units for 4–6; good availability even over NY, ¥40,000–70,000/night.
- **Kinosaki:** one 6-person suite or 2–3 rooms in one ryokan; NY = highest rate, 3–6 months lead.
- **Kōyasan:** shukubō rooms are 2–4 tatami rooms; take two or three rooms.
- **Arima / Kobe:** ryokan rooms for 6 exist (Zuien, Gekkōen); Kobe city hotels easy.

### Suggested shapes

- **3 nights (Kyoto only, 30 Dec–2 Jan):** 30 Nishiki + Gion walk; 31 rest, Okera-mairi 18:00, Chion-in for the four; 1 Jan Tōdai-ji at 07:00 + Nara deer, early back; 2 Jan Ōhara or Uji.
- **4 nights:** above + 1 night Kōyasan (pre-NY, e.g. 28–29 Dec) or a Kinosaki crab night.
- **5 nights:** Kyoto 3 (30 Dec–2 Jan) → Kinosaki 2 (2–4 Jan; crab, baths; Himeji on the way back). Fly out of ITM or ride the Nozomi.

### Downsides / honest notes

- Kyoto 30 Dec–3 Jan is the city's **most expensive week**; machiya and good restaurants either close or sell out by October.
- Chion-in means 3 h standing in the cold; the partner should not do it.
- Kinosaki's seven baths are currently five; crab plans at the top inns can exceed ¥50,000 pp.
- Kōyasan rooms are cold and shared facilities are common; one night is right, two is a lot.
- Himeji closed exactly when a Kyoto-based group would naturally go (29–30 Dec).
- Kobe Luminarie and Tōka Ebisu both fall outside the realistic window; do not plan around them.

**Split-group opportunities:** 31 Dec (Chion-in queue vs Okera-mairi + kaiseki; or USJ Countdown for brother/SIL); Kurama ridge walk vs Kibune onsen; Fushimi summit vs lower loop; Himeji keep vs garden.

## 2. Setouchi (Naoshima/Teshima/Inujima · Okayama · Kurashiki · Onomichi/Shimanami · Hiroshima · Miyajima · Iwakuni · Matsuyama/Dōgo · Takamatsu/Ritsurin · Kotohira)

### Why these dates

- **Oysters at peak.** Hiroshima oysters are fattest Jan–Feb; kakigoya (oyster huts) on Miyajima and at Hiroshima port run all winter. The **Miyajima Oyster Festival is a February event** (2nd weekend; 2026 edition was cancelled for lack of supply) — not in window. (jeepe.jp, miyajima.or.jp)
- **Mild, dry, bright.** Setouchi is the sunniest part of Japan in winter: 3–11 °C, little rain, calm seas most days. Cycling and ferries are reliable.
- **Dreamination** (Hiroshima's 1.4 M-light illumination, Peace Boulevard) ran 17 Nov 2025 – 3 Jan 2026; expect the same window in 2026-27. (travel.rakuten.com)
- **New Year free days:** Ritsurin Garden and Okayama Kōrakuen are free on 1 Jan with koto concerts and crane walks (Kōrakuen 1–3 Jan "Early Spring Festival"). (setouchiexplorer.com, okayama-korakuen.jp)
- **Closures to design around:** Peace Memorial Museum 30–31 Dec; Okayama Castle 29–31 Dec; Ōhara Museum 28–31 Dec (main gallery only 1 Jan); Benesse Art Site: Teshima Art Museum closed 31 Dec – 2 Jan in 2025-26 and Teshima's town bus and bike rentals stop over NY; Chichu runs short hours 31 Dec; **annual maintenance closures hit Chichu, Benesse House and others ~13–19 Jan 2026** — in 2027 that will again be after the trip but verify. Naoshima New Museum of Art (Ando, opened 31 May 2025) is the new anchor. (benesse-artsite.jp)

### Experience cards

**ST-01 · Naoshima: Chichu, Benesse House, novo museu / Naoshima art day**
Chichu (Monet/Turrell/De Maria; timed online tickets, ¥2,100, closed Mon), Lee Ufan, Benesse House Museum, new Naoshima New Museum of Art (¥1,500), Yayoi Kusama pumpkins. Island buses + shuttle; Chichu has ramps but some stairs; Benesse House has a lift. Cold wind on the coast. Ferry from Uno (20 min) or Takamatsu (50 min).
Suits: all. Bump 4 (ferry + bus; sit-down museums; order a taxi on-island for the partner — only ~5 exist, prebook). Effort 2. Mixed. ¥6,000–8,000 all museums. Full day. Chichu tickets 1–2 months ahead; Benesse House hotel 4–6 months. NY: Chichu short hours 31 Dec; most sites closed 1 Jan; confirm the 2026-27 calendar when posted (Nov). Uniqueness 5. (benesse-artsite.jp, japan-guide.com)

**ST-02 · Museu de Arte de Teshima / Teshima Art Museum (Naitō/Nishizawa)**
A concrete shell with water droplets; silent, barefoot, 30–60 min. Hilly island; electric bikes or the town bus (bus and bike rentals **stop over NY**). Closed Tue in winter + 31 Dec–2 Jan.
Suits: all, but logistics are the issue. Bump 3 (ferry + bus; the museum itself is flat). Effort 2. Indoor. ¥1,570. Half day. Timed ticket 1–2 months. NY: closed 31 Dec–2 Jan (2025-26 pattern). Uniqueness 5. (benesse-artsite.jp)

**ST-03 · Inujima Seirensho / Inujima Seirensho Art Museum**
Copper-refinery ruins turned museum; tiny, car-free island. Winter: ferries thin out and the museum has winter closures (closed Tue–Thu Dec–Feb in recent years; and over NY). Only worth it on a 2-night Naoshima stay.
Suits: brother/SIL, parents. Bump 2. Effort 2. Mixed. ¥2,100. Half day. Check calendar. NY: likely closed. Uniqueness 4. Honest: cut unless art is the trip's theme.

**ST-04 · Kurashiki Bikan e Museu Ōhara / Kurashiki canal quarter**
Flat willow canal, Edo warehouses, Ōhara Museum (El Greco, Monet) — **closed 28–31 Dec; main gallery only 1 Jan; full from 2 Jan**. Denim street. 15 min by train from Okayama.
Suits: all. Bump 5. Effort 1. Mixed. ¥2,000 museum. 3 h. No booking. NY: streets open; museum closed 28–31 Dec. Uniqueness 3. (gltjp.com)

**ST-05 · Kōrakuen no Ano Novo / Okayama Kōrakuen New Year (1–3 Jan)**
Free entry 1 Jan, koto concert, red-crowned cranes walked in the garden 11:00–14:00; kyōgen 2 Jan, tea 3 Jan. Castle (black "crow castle") closed 29–31 Dec, open 1 Jan. Flat garden, a 10-min walk from the castle.
Suits: all. Bump 5. Effort 1. Outdoor. Free (1 Jan) / ¥500. 2 h. No booking. NY: the event. Uniqueness 4 (cranes at NY only). (okayama-korakuen.jp)

**ST-06 · Shimanami Kaidō de bicicleta (trecho curto) / Shimanami Kaidō short ride (Onomichi → Ikuchijima)**
Rent at Onomichi (public terminals ¥3,000/day, 08:00–18:00 in winter; e-bikes from Giant/Cyclonoie). Mukaishima + Innoshima + Ikuchijima is 30 km flat-ish with bridge ramps; return by ferry/bus. Many cafés close 29 Dec–3 Jan; the Onomichi Mukaishima terminal keeps a NY schedule. The partner does Onomichi's temple-walk ropeway and the Setoda ferry instead.
Suits: fit four. Bump 1 (ride) / 4 (ferry sightseeing). Effort 4. Outdoor. ¥3,000–7,000 bike. Full day. Prebook e-bikes 2–4 weeks; NY week dates fill. NY: rentals mostly open; cafés closed. Uniqueness 4. (cyclonoie.com, jb-honshi.co.jp)

**ST-07 · Onomichi: ladeira dos templos e teleférico / Onomichi temple hillside + Senkō-ji ropeway**
Ropeway up (¥700 return), 1.5 km downhill lanes with cats and viewpoints; many steps on the way down — partner rides both ways and walks the flat shōtengai. Good overnight base (Onomichi U2 hotel in a cycle warehouse).
Suits: all. Bump 3. Effort 3. Outdoor. ¥700. 3 h. No booking. NY: open. Uniqueness 3.

**ST-08 · Hiroshima: Parque e Museu da Paz / Peace Memorial Park & Museum**
Museum **closed 30–31 Dec**, open 1 Jan; the park, Dome and cenotaph are open 24 h. Flat. Allow 2 h in the museum (benches, lifts). Pair with Dreamination (to ~3 Jan) and oysters at Kakifune Kanawa or a port kakigoya.
Suits: all. Bump 5. Effort 1. Indoor. ¥200. 3 h. No booking (timed entry on busy days). NY: closed 30–31 Dec. Uniqueness 5. (hpmmuseum.jp)

**ST-09 · Miyajima: Itsukushima e ostras / Miyajima + Itsukushima + grilled oysters**
JR + 10-min ferry (JR ferry free with pass). Shrine open 06:30–17:00 in winter; 1–3 Jan is Miyajima's hatsumode (big but manageable vs Fushimi). Kakiya and Omotesandō oyster grills. Mt Misen ropeway (¥2,000 return) + 30 min of rocky steps to the summit — fit four only. Check the tide table: the "floating" torii needs high tide; low tide lets you walk to it.
Suits: all (ropeway + summit for the four). Bump 4 (flat waterfront; 10-min ferry). Effort 1 / 4 summit. Outdoor. ¥500 shrine + ¥3,000 oysters. Half–full day. Ryokan on-island (Iwasō, Kinsuikan) 3–4 months for NY. NY: open; crowded 1–3 Jan. Uniqueness 5. (dive-hiroshima.com, japanactivity.com)

**ST-10 · Ponte Kintai-kyō, Iwakuni / Iwakuni Kintai-kyō bridge**
Five wooden arches; walking it is steep stairs on each arch (partner can watch from the bank). White snakes museum. 50 min from Hiroshima; combine with Miyajima (same line) rather than a separate day.
Suits: all for the view; crossing for the four. Bump 3. Effort 3. Outdoor. ¥310. 1.5 h. No booking. NY: open. Uniqueness 3.

**ST-11 · Dōgo Onsen Honkan (Matsuyama) / Dōgo Onsen Honkan**

## 3. Kyushu (Fukuoka · Yufuin · Beppu · Kurokawa · Takachiho · Nagasaki · Kumamoto · Aso · Kagoshima/Sakurajima · Ibusuki · Yakushima)

### Why these dates

- **Takachiho Yokagura season** (all-night village kagura, 33 dances) runs mid-Nov – early Feb on Saturday nights in ~20 hamlets; the 2026-27 hamlet calendar is published on a rolling basis in autumn. The tourist version at Takachiho Shrine runs nightly 20:00–21:00 (¥1,000) **except 31 Dec–1 Jan**. (japan-event.info, takachiho-kanko.info)
- **Kurokawa Onsen Yu Akari** bamboo-lantern illumination: 20 Dec 2025 – 31 Mar 2026 pattern; expect from ~19–20 Dec 2026, sunset–22:00, free. (travel.rakuten.com)
- **Hatsumode at Dazaifu Tenmangu** > 2 M visitors 1–3 Jan — the biggest in Kyushu; tours substitute other shrines 28 Dec–5 Jan because of congestion. (dazaifu.org, getyourguide.com)
- **Yatai** (Nakasu/Tenjin stalls, ~100 licensed) run 18:00–02:00 by owner's choice; a minority open 31 Dec–1 Jan, most back by 3–4 Jan. Mild winter (4–12 °C) makes open-air stalls fine. (japan-guide.com)
- **Winter oddities in your favour:** Yakushima's Arakawa trailhead road is open to private cars only Dec–Feb (no mandatory shuttle); Kumamoto Castle opened 1 Jan free with a samurai gate-opening in 2026. Nagasaki Lantern Festival is **5–21 Feb 2027** — out. (yakumonkey.com, x.com/kumamoto_castle, discover-nagasaki.com)
- **Aso caveat:** crater viewing has been closed since Jan 2026 (helicopter crash, then alert level 2–3); as of Sept 2026 the summit plaza is reachable but the 1-km crater zone is still closed; winter road/snow closures add to that. Kusasenri, Daikanbō and the museum are open. (watabi.org)

### Experience cards

**KY-01 · Yatai de Fukuoka / Fukuoka yatai crawl (Nakasu riverside)**
8–10 seats per stall; ramen, yakitori, mentaiko tamagoyaki, oden. Six people split across two stalls. Open-air but under a roof; the partner sits on the end stool. 28–30 Dec and 2–5 Jan are safe; 31 Dec–1 Jan patchy.
Suits: all. Bump 4. Effort 1. Outdoor-covered. ¥3,000–5,000. 2 h. No booking. NY: patchy 31–1. Uniqueness 4. (japanactivity.com)

**KY-02 · Hatsumōde em Dazaifu / Dazaifu Tenmangu New Year**
Plum-blossom shrine of the scholar god; umegae-mochi stalls, flat 500-m approach. 1–3 Jan is a 2 M-person crush — go 31 Dec morning (osechi stalls up, quiet), or 4–5 Jan. 30 min from Tenjin by Nishitetsu.
Suits: all. Bump 2 (1–3 Jan) / 5 (other days). Effort 1. Outdoor. Free. 2.5 h. No booking. NY: peak crowd. Uniqueness 4 at NY, 3 otherwise. (dazaifu.org)

**KY-03 · Yufuin pelo trem Yufuin-no-Mori / Yufuin via the Yufuin no Mori train + Lake Kinrin**
All-reserved sightseeing express Hakata → Yufuin 2 h 15 (3 per day); 6 seats must be booked exactly 1 month out for 28 Dec–4 Jan. Yufuin: flat 1.5 km main street to the misty lake at dawn, boutique ryokan (Tamanoyu, Sansō Murata, Kamenoi Bessō) with private baths.
Suits: all. Bump 5. Effort 1. Mixed. Train ¥5,190 + ryokan ¥30,000–60,000 pp. 1 night. Train 1 month, ryokan 3–4 months. NY: open; premium. Uniqueness 4. (jrpass.com)

**KY-04 · Infernos de Beppu / Beppu Jigoku Meguri + jigoku-mushi steaming**
Seven "hells" (¥2,400 combo), flat except Umi-jigoku's small slopes; steam-cook your own lunch at Jigoku-mushi Kōbō. Open 365 days. Partner can take the hot-spring foot baths; avoid the 100 °C steam rooms. 1 h from Yufuin by bus.
Suits: all. Bump 4. Effort 1. Outdoor. ¥2,400 + ¥1,500 lunch. Half day. No booking. NY: open. Uniqueness 3.

**KY-05 · Kurokawa Onsen e as lanternas Yu Akari / Kurokawa Onsen + Yu Akari lanterns**
Riverside hamlet with ~30 ryokan; ¥1,500 tegata pass for 3 rotenburo (many are hot/outdoor and stepped — the partner should use the ryokan's kashikiri bath). Bamboo-lantern illumination nightly from ~20 Dec to end-March. No train: bus from Fukuoka (2 h 45) or Aso/Kumamoto. Steep little lanes.
Suits: all for the night; baths-hopping for the four. Bump 3 (bus; slopes). Effort 2. Mixed. ¥30,000–50,000 pp. 1 night. Ryokan 3–4 months (NY). NY: open. Uniqueness 5. (travel.rakuten.com)

**KY-06 · Yokagura de Takachiho (noite inteira na aldeia) / Takachiho village Yokagura (Saturday all-nighter)**
Sit on tatami in a farmhouse from ~19:00 to dawn with locals, shōchū and 33 dances; ¥3,000–5,000 donation. In window: Saturdays 26 Dec and 2 Jan if a hamlet is dancing (confirm on the Takachiho tourism schedule in Nov). Fallback: the 20:00 shrine show (not 31 Dec–1 Jan). Access is bus-only: Kumamoto → Takachiho 3 h 10 (1 direct bus a day) or Nobeoka 50 min; realistically **rent a car** (Kumamoto or Fukuoka) for Takachiho + Aso + Kurokawa.
Suits: the fit four for the all-nighter; the partner does the shrine show and the gorge boardwalk. Bump 2 (all-night) / 4 (shrine show). Effort 3. Indoor. ¥1,000–5,000. Overnight / 1 h. Lodging in Takachiho 2–3 months. NY: hamlet dates vary; shrine show off 31–1. Uniqueness 5. (japan-event.info, takachiho-kanko.info)

**KY-07 · Garganta de Takachiho / Takachiho Gorge boat + boardwalk**
Rowboats under the Manai falls (¥4,100–5,100 per boat for 30 min, 3 per boat; queue system, suspended after rain), 1 km boardwalk with stairs at both ends. Winter is low-season — short queues.
Suits: all (boat: brother rows). Bump 3. Effort 2. Outdoor. ¥1,500 pp. 2 h. Boat ticket same day. NY: open. Uniqueness 4. (takachihogorge.com)

**KY-08 · Caldeira de Aso no inverno / Aso caldera (Kusasenri, Daikanbō) — no crater**
Grassland and caldera-rim panoramas with snow dusting; Volcano Museum. Crater itself closed (alert level 2, 1 km zone) with no reopening date, plus winter road closures. Car or Kyushu Sanko bus from Aso station.
Suits: all as a drive-through. Bump 4 by car. Effort 1. Outdoor. ¥1,100 museum. 3 h. No booking. NY: open. Uniqueness 2 while the crater is shut. Honest: cut unless the crater reopens by Dec. (watabi.org)

**KY-09 · Castelo de Kumamoto a 1 de janeiro / Kumamoto Castle New Year opening**
2025-26: closed only 29 Dec; open 30–31 Dec via North Entrance; 1 Jan 09:00 ceremonial gate-opening by the samurai troupe and free entry. Earthquake-rebuilt keep has a lift to the top. Sakuranobaba Jōsaien food court next door.
Suits: all. Bump 4 (lift). Effort 2. Mixed. ¥800 (free 1 Jan). 2.5 h. No booking. NY: open except ~29 Dec. Uniqueness 3. (x.com/kumamoto_castle)

**KY-10 · Nagasaki: Glover, Dejima, bonde, champon / Nagasaki city day**
Glover Garden (escalators up the hill, open daily), Dejima (open year-round), Atomic Bomb Museum (closed 29–31 Dec), Chinatown champon, Mt Inasa ropeway night view (one of Japan's "three"). Flat tram city; Glover is a hill with moving walkways.
Suits: all. Bump 4. Effort 2. Mixed. ¥620 + ¥520 + ¥200. Full day. No booking. NY: museum closed 29–31 Dec; rest open. Uniqueness 4. (japan-guide.com, kanpai-japan.com)

**KY-11 · Gunkanjima / Hashima "Battleship Island" cruise**
Landing allowed only under 0.5 m waves / 5 m/s wind; Yamasa says landings succeed ~100 days a year, and winter has higher cancellation and cruise-only rates. 3 h, ¥4,000–5,000. Pregnant guests are accepted on the boat but the landing walkway is uneven.
Suits: brother/SIL, parents. Bump 2. Effort 2. Outdoor. ¥4,500. 3 h. Book 2–4 weeks; have a backup. NY: operators run most days, weather-dependent. Uniqueness 4. Honest: a coin-flip in winter. (gunkanjima-cruise.jp, japanactivity.com)

**KY-12 · Sakurajima e Sengan-en / Kagoshima: Sakurajima ferry + Sengan-en**
24-hour ferry every 15–20 min, 15 min crossing; lava trail and Yunohira observatory (bus). Sengan-en garden on the bay facing the volcano; kurobuta tonkatsu and shirokuma shaved ice. Shiroyama lookout by City View bus.
Suits: all. Bump 5. Effort 1–2. Outdoor. ¥250 ferry + ¥1,600 Sengan-en. Half–full day. No booking. NY: open (Sengan-en NY events 1–3 Jan). Uniqueness 4. (visitor.sakurajima.gr.jp)

**KY-13 · Banho de areia de Ibusuki / Ibusuki sand bath (NOT for the partner)**
Buried in 50–55 °C volcanic sand for 10 min at Saraku. Facilities refuse pregnant guests and anyone with blood-pressure/heart issues. 50 min by train from Kagoshima; Ibusuki Hakusuikan has rooms for 6 and its own sand bath.
Suits: the five, not the partner (she can do a foot bath/lunch). Bump 1. Effort 1. Indoor. ¥1,100–1,500. 1 h. No booking (group of 5 at peak: call ahead). NY: open. Uniqueness 4. (samurai-sauna-magazine.com)

**KY-14 · Yakushima no inverno / Yakushima (Shiratani Unsuikyō or Jōmon Sugi)**
From Kagoshima: JAC 35 min (3/day) or Toppy jetfoil 1 h 50 (2–3/day in winter, ~6 listed on the winter timetable, cancels in rough seas). Jōmon Sugi: 22 km, 10–12 h, 04:30 start, Arakawa road open to cars Dec–Feb — parents/brother only, with a guide. Shiratani (mossy "Mononoke" forest) 2–3 h loop is the family option; the partner can do the first 30-min section. Rainy island ("35 days of rain a month"); 8–14 °C at sea level, snow above 1,000 m. ## 4. Hokkaido (Sapporo · Otaru · Niseko · Furano/Biei · Noboribetsu/Jōzankei · Hakodate)

### Why these dates — and why mostly not

- **What is actually on 20 Dec – 9 Jan:**
  - Sapporo White Illumination: Odori Park section **ends 25 Dec**; the Ekimae-dōri, Minami 1-jō and Sapporo station sections run to mid-Feb/14 Mar. (sapporo.travel, jw-webmagazine.com)
  - Munich Christmas Market in Sapporo: **20 Nov – 25 Dec 2026**, Odori 1–2-chōme, 11:00–21:00 (official 2026 dates). (sapporo.travel)
  - Hakodate Christmas Fantasy: 29 Nov – 25 Dec (2025 pattern), fireworks on 21/24/25 Dec. (hakodate.travel)
  - Shirogane Blue Pond illumination (Biei): **22 Oct 2026 – 21 Apr 2027**, Dec from 16:30, Jan from 17:00, to 21:00. (biei-hokkaido.jp)
  - Furano Ningle Terrace lights: 29 Nov – 31 Mar, 12:00–20:45.
  - Hokkaidō Jingū hatsumode 1–3 Jan (largest in Hokkaido, ~1 M).
  - **Otaru Snow Light Path is 6–13 Feb 2027 — confirmed outside the window.** Sapporo Snow Festival is early Feb; Jōzankei Snow Candle Way late Jan–Feb; Lake Shikotsu Ice Festival late Jan. (japan-event.info, travel.rakuten.com)
- **Snow reliability:** Sapporo averages 48 cm on the ground by late Dec and 85 cm in Jan; Niseko and Furano are reliably open from 28 Nov 2026 (Grand Hirafu, Furano). White Christmas and white New Year are near-certain. (japanactivity.com, uchijapan.com)
- **Cold for a pregnant traveller:** Sapporo Dec mean −1.8 °C (lows −5), Jan mean −4.8 °C (lows −8 to −10); mountain onsen towns −10 to −20 °C. The real hazard is **ice underfoot** (compacted, polished pavements in Sapporo and Otaru) — a fall at 30 weeks is the thing to avoid; strap-on spikes and short walks only. (japanhighlights.com, ryokanfinder.com)
- **Flights:** HND↔CTS is the world's second-busiest route, 56–66 nonstop/day (ANA, JAL, Skymark, AirDo); 1 h 35; fares ¥12,000–45,000 at NY, cheap in mid-Jan. Shinkansen Tokyo → Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto 3 h 58 (Hayabusa, ¥22,690–24,200), + 20 min to Hakodate; Sapporo by rail is 7.5–8 h (extension to Sapporo not before the 2030s). (flightsfrom.com, nippon.com)

Ropeway only in winter (road closed 9 Nov – 12 Apr 2027), 10:00–21:00; one of Japan's three night views, −5 °C on the deck. Morning market 06:00–14:00 Jan–Apr, open through NY by stall; Goryōkaku star fort (tower has a lift). Reach by Hayabusa Shinkansen 4 h from Tokyo (bump 4) or 1 h 20 flight HKD↔HND (~8/day).
Suits: all. Bump 4. Effort 1. Mixed. ¥1,800 ropeway. 1–2 nights. No booking. NY: open; Christmas Fantasy ended 25 Dec. Uniqueness 4. (watabi.org/hakodate-guide, visit-hokkaido.jp)

**HK-10 · Hokkaidō Jingū no Ano Novo / Hokkaidō Jingū hatsumode in the snow (1–3 Jan)**
Hokkaido's biggest shrine, in Maruyama Park's snowy forest; ~1 M visitors over 3 days, but it's a wide flat approach. Subway to Maruyama-kōen + 15 min walk on packed snow.
Suits: all. Bump 3 (ice). Effort 2. Outdoor. Free. 2 h. No booking. NY: the event. Uniqueness 3.

### Bases for six and lodging outlook

- **Sapporo (Odori/Susukino):** plenty of 2-bed condo hotels (Mimaru, Tokyu Stay, Hotel Mystays Premier) and connecting rooms; NY rates ¥40,000–80,000/night for 6; book 2–3 months. Everything (subway, underground Chikaho walkway from Sapporo station to Susukino) can be done without stepping on ice — the key advantage for the partner.
- **Niseko:** chalets for 6 are the NY unicorn — book by June; or Hirafu hotel rooms (Skye, Ki Niseko) ¥80,000+/room.
- **Furano/Biei:** Prince Hotel, pensions; 2 rooms minimum.
- **Hakodate:** Yunokawa onsen hotels (rooms for 4–5) or bay-area hotels; easy.
- **Noboribetsu/Jōzankei:** large ryokan, 6-person rooms exist (Furukawa, Takimotokan); 3 months.

### Suggested shapes

- **3 nights (Sapporo-only, 20–23 Dec as the trip's opener):** fly HND→CTS on arrival day +1; Christmas market, Odori lights, Otaru, crab; Jōzankei day. Then CTS→ITM/KIX (1 h 55, ~10/day) to start Kansai.
- **4 nights:** Sapporo 2 + Noboribetsu 1 + Hakodate 1; Shinkansen back to Tokyo (4 h + 20 min) — bump 3, or fly HKD→HND.
- **5 nights:** Sapporo 2 → Niseko 3 (fit four ski; partner, planner and a parent rotate to Jōzankei/Sapporo) — split-base, two taxis.

### Downsides / honest notes

- The headline Hokkaido events (Snow Festival, Otaru lights, Jōzankei candles, Lake Shikotsu) are **all in late Jan–Feb**. In this window Hokkaido offers snow, seafood, onsen and skiing, not festivals.
- Sapporo's Christmas content ends 25 Dec; 26 Dec – 9 Jan it is a cold city with illuminated streets.
- Ice is the real risk for the partner; every outdoor card above is shorter and slower than it reads.
- Niseko at NY is the most expensive lodging in Japan; chalets for 6 may already be gone for 2026-27.
- Adding Hokkaido costs two flights and ~1.5 days of transit; if the trip already has Kansai + one of Setouchi/Kyushu, Hokkaido is the region to drop.

**Split-group opportunities:** Niseko ski vs Sapporo/Jōzankei; Mt Hakodate at night vs hotel bath; Blue Pond night drive vs Furano terrace.
