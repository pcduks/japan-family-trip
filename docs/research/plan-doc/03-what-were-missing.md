# What we're missing

**Three regions you haven't seen would add something genuinely new without breaking the pace rules.** Each one replaces a stop rather than adding days:

- **The snow monkeys and onsen street of Yudanaka**, in Nagano.
- **The art islands and gardens around Okayama**, including Naoshima.
- **The crab-and-onsen town of Kinosaki.**

Hokkaido, northern Tohoku, Kyushu and Okinawa are worth a trip of their own, not a squeeze into this one.

The easiest gains are experiences rather than places: a geiko afternoon in Kanazawa, a single act of kabuki, Kyoto's New Year rituals, and sumo. The sumo tournament starts the day after you fly home.

## Swaps that work

Each swap keeps 20 nights, keeps every stay at two nights or more, and keeps the New Year block exactly as it is. Only the stretch before 29 Dec or after 4 Jan changes, so no move lands on a peak travel day.

| Version | Changes | You gain | You give up | Moves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Current plan** | — | — | — | 6 |
| **+ Snow monkeys** | Tokyo 20–23, Yudanaka 23–25, Kanazawa 25–28, then as now | Snow monkeys, an onsen-street night before Christmas, and Obuse or Matsumoto | A Tokyo day, and Kanazawa's fourth night (go to the samurai house on 25 Dec, since it closes 26–27) | 7 |
| **Art instead of Fuji** | Kyoto 1–5, Okayama 5–7 (Himeji on the way), Tokyo 7–9 | Chichu Art Museum on Naoshima on Wed 6 Jan, the Ohara Museum, Korakuen and Himeji | The Hakone ryokan, the Hakone museums and two Fuji dawns. You still see Fuji from the shinkansen on 7 Jan | 6 |
| **Both** | The two swaps above together | The most new places: five of the seven places would be new to you two | A Tokyo day, Kanazawa's fourth night, and Hakone | 7 |
| **Crab and onsen instead of Fuji** | Kyoto 1–5, Kinosaki 5–7, Tokyo 7–9 | A classic onsen town in crab season | Hakone and Fuji, plus a 5–6 h trip back to Tokyo on 7 Jan | 6 |

Of these, **Art instead of Fuji** fits your interests best, because you've said museums and gardens matter. **Snow monkeys** is the most memorable single sight, but it depends on enough snow by 23 Dec and on her feeling steady on the forest path.
