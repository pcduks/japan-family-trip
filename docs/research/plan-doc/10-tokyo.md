# Tokyo

**Sun 20 – Thu 24 Dec and Thu 7 – Sat 9 Jan.** Mon 21 Dec is a closing day for most museums, so it is the market and gardens day. Some of the best exhibitions only fit the January stay. Neighbourhoods, izakaya tips and Pedro's own bar and restaurant list are in the earlier [Tokyo for Six guide](https://claude.ai/artifact/WGmgutnpVdHdwH9iAmxuyi).

## Before you plan

- **[teamLab Planets](https://www.teamlab.art/e/planets/):** you walk barefoot through knee-deep water, and teamLab says *pregnant women aren't permitted in some areas*. Tickets are online only. [teamLab Borderless](https://www.teamlab.art/e/borderless-azabudai/) is dry and easier for her.
- **[Tokyo National Museum](https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_free_page/index.php?id=113&lang=en):** the main building is mostly closed 22–25 Dec. Go on 7–8 Jan instead; it is open to 20:00 on Fri 8 Jan.
- **[Tokyo Metropolitan Art Museum](https://www.tobikan.jp/en/guide/index.html)** is closed 21–28 Dec. Its Musée d'Orsay show is therefore a 7–8 Jan visit.
- **The four Edo-period gardens** run by the city (Hama-rikyu, Rikugien, Koishikawa Korakuen, Kiyosumi) are open 9:00–17:00 on all your dates. They close only 29 Dec – 1 Jan ([city leaflet](https://www.kensetsu.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/documents/d/kensetsu/070106teienpanhueigo)).
- **Ghibli Museum** tickets go on sale on the 10th of the month before: 10 Nov for December and 10 Dec for January ([guide](https://jw-webmagazine.com/tips/best-ways-to-get-ghibli-museum-tickets/)). It is closed on Tuesdays.
