# Takayama and the Hida mountains

**Option A: Takayama Mon 28 – Wed 30 Dec (2 nights), then Gero Onsen Wed 30 Dec – Fri 1 Jan (2 nights).** Two nights in Takayama is enough: [japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5912_takayama_full.html) plans it as a single day and [OneDayAway](https://onedayawaytravel.com/?p=2688) recommends two days. Okuhida is option B, or a day trip on 29 Dec.

## Before you plan

- **[Takayama Jinya](https://jinya.gifu.jp/en/guide/info/) closes 29 Dec – 3 Jan**, so see it on the afternoon of 28 Dec.
- **Weekday closures:** [Kusakabe House](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5903.html) is closed Tuesdays, which rules out 29 Dec. Yoshijima House is closed Monday to Thursday in winter.
- **[Hida Folk Village](https://www.hidanosato.com/) is open every day of the year.** It has a Christmas light-up 19–25 Dec 2026.
- **Snow:** Takayama averages 66 cm of snowfall in December, and Gero is about 2°C milder ([Takayama climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takayama,_Gifu), [Gero climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gero,_Gifu)).
- **Getting around:** Takayama to Gero takes 45 minutes by limited express ([japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5990.html)). One direct Hida train a day runs to Kyoto, leaving Gero at 16:18 and arriving about 17:45 ([osakastation.com](https://www.osakastation.com/limited-express-hida-gifu-gero-takayama/); check the 2027 timetable). Otherwise go via Nagoya: 90 minutes, then 35 minutes on the shinkansen.
- **Onsen in pregnancy:** Japan's Environment Ministry says pregnant women can bathe, but should keep it short and not too hot ([official guide, PDF](https://www.env.go.jp/content/900492871.pdf)). Water at 40°C or below and about 10 minutes is the usual advice ([Japan Ryokan Guide](https://www.japanryokanguide.com/en/blog/onsen-while-pregnant)). Get her doctor's OK first.

## Gero Onsen

- **What to do:** three public bathhouses, free footbaths and the riverside Funsenike bath. A ¥1,300 [Yumeguri Tegata pass](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5990.html) gets you into three ryokan baths.
- **[Gero Onsen Gassho Village](https://www.gero-gassho.jp/lg_en/):** thatched houses and folk crafts, open 9:00–16:00 on 31 Dec and 1–2 Jan.
- **Where to stay:**
  - [Yunoshimakan](https://selected-ryokan.com/ryokan/yunoshimakan_gero_gifu_nagoya-chubu-hokuriku.html): a 1931 building, four free private baths and dinner in your room.
  - [Suimeikan](https://suimeikan.co.jp/english/onsen): 3 minutes from the station, with private baths to book and some rooms with their own onsen.
  - [Bosenkan](https://www.japanryokanguide.com/en/blog/best-onsen-ryokans-near-nagoya): only about 8 rooms have open-air baths, and they sell out first.
  - Ask each one about its 31 Dec dinner and New Year breakfast plan.
- Links: [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Gero%20Onsen) · [Photos](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Gero%20Onsen%20winter) · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/geroonsen/) · [Video: winter holiday in Gero Onsen](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QZfQNPIF8LE)

## Okuhida, if you choose option B

- About 1 hour by bus from Takayama to Hirayu, or 90 minutes to Shin-Hotaka, roughly hourly. A two-day ticket costs ¥4,400 ([japan-guide](https://japan-guide.com/e/e5941.html)).
- Ryokan with open-air baths in the rooms: [Miyama Ouan](https://selected-ryokan.com/ryokan/miyama-ouan_oku-hida_gifu_nagoya-chubu-hokuriku.html) and [Kutsuroginoya Yu](https://selected-ryokan.com/ryokan/kutsuroginoya-yu_oku-hida_gifu_nagoya-chubu-hokuriku.html) (¥27,000–60,000 per person).
- [Hirayu no Mori](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5942.html) has outdoor baths in the snow and a private bath for ¥3,000 per 45 minutes.
- Links: [@okuhidagram](https://www.instagram.com/okuhidagram/) (official) · [Okuhida YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC8RBvsN1tvg5Seift-hWGcw)
