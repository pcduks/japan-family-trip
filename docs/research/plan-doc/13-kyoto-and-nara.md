# Kyoto and Nara

**Fri 1 – Tue 5 Jan, 4 nights.** Shrines and temples stay open over New Year. Many shops and restaurants close on 1 Jan, and some museums stay closed until 4 Jan. Mon 4 Jan is a normal Monday closing day, so that is the Nara day. Day plans and neighbourhood notes are in the earlier [Kyoto for Six guide](https://claude.ai/artifact/R5h7gGy8NcavyHmpNRJ68u).

## What's open over New Year

These dates come from the 2025–26 season. The 2026–27 lists are not out yet.

- **Returning holiday traffic peaks 2–4 Jan, worst on the 3rd** ([japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e2276.html)). Reserve seats for the Nara trip.
- **Closed through 4 Jan:** Kyoto Imperial Palace, the Katsura and Shugakuin villas, the Botanical Garden and Gion Corner ([Kyoto tourist office closure list, PDF](https://global.kyoto.travel/resource/global/download/162-pdf.pdf)).
- **[Kyoto National Museum](https://www.kyohaku.go.jp/eng/exhibitions/feature/b/2026_sheep/)** opens on 2–3 Jan for its Year of the Sheep exhibition, and is closed on 4 Jan.
- **Nishiki Market:** many shops close 1–3 Jan ([official site](https://www.kyoto-nishiki.or.jp/en/contact/)), so go on 4 Jan.
- **Department stores** were closed 1–2 Jan last year; Kyoto Station restaurants were open 2–3 Jan ([The Tokyo Chapter](https://www.thetokyochapter.com/?p=19044)). On 1 Jan, hotel restaurants are the safe bet.
- **Events:**
  - [Yasaka Shrine](https://kyotokankoyagi.com/yasaka-hatsumode-en): Okera fire ritual on New Year's Eve (the rite at 19:00, then the fire from about 19:30 on 31 Dec to 05:00 on 1 Jan); court-costume card game on 3 Jan.
  - [Kitano Tenmangū](https://leafkyoto.net/en/221125-kyoto-hatsumode): first-calligraphy festival on 2 Jan, with a tea room.
  - Heian Jingū: open 6:00–18:30 on 2–3 Jan.
- Honke Owariya, the famous soba shop, was [reported closed in August 2026](https://www.insidekyoto.com/honke-owariya-restaurant-kyoto).
