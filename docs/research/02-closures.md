# Japan year-end / New Year operating status, 28 Dec 2026 – 5 Jan 2027

Researched 1 Oct 2026. Calendar: Thu 31 Dec 2026 (Ōmisoka), **Fri 1 Jan 2027 (only statutory holiday)**, Sat 2 – Sun 3 Jan, **Mon 4 Jan = first working day**. Because 2–3 Jan fall on a weekend this year, the "shutdown" is effectively Tue 29 Dec – Sun 3 Jan with many offices/independent shops not back until Mon 4 or Tue 5 Jan.

**Confidence key**
- **A** = 2026/27 dates officially announced by the venue (as of 1 Oct 2026).
- **B** = venue is "open every day of the year" by policy, or follows a fixed, multi-year pattern (e.g., "closed 29 Dec–3 Jan" every year); very likely to repeat.
- **C** = based on the 2025/26 (or 2024/25) pattern only; venue sets dates each November/December — re-verify ~1–15 Dec 2026.

Column codes: **O** = Open, **C** = Closed, **R** = Reduced (short hours / partial / fewer shops). Dates are 28 Dec → 5 Jan.

**Note for 20–27 Dec:** essentially everything runs normal (often *extended*) hours; Christmas is not a holiday. Watch for: Tokyo National Museum partial from 22 Dec and closed from 26 Dec; Ghibli Museum closed from 27 Dec; Ohara Museum (Kurashiki) historically closed ~23–27 Dec for cleaning; Kinosaki ropeway closed 25 Dec; Imperial Palace East Gardens closed from 28 Dec; markets (Tsukiji/Toyosu/Nishiki/Kuromon/Ameyoko/Ōmichō) are at their *busiest* 28–30 Dec.
**Note for 6–9 Jan:** nearly everything is back to normal by Tue 5–Wed 6 Jan. Watch for: Toyosu produce section closed 6 Jan; Naoshima/Benesse winter maintenance begins ~mid-Jan (Chichu closed 12 Jan–19 Mar 2027 — announced); Hakone Ropeway annual inspection usually starts mid-Jan (2026: 13 Jan–20 Feb, sections alternate, replacement bus); Kaiyukan maintenance closure mid-Jan (2026: 14–15 Jan); Yokohama Red Brick full closure one weekday mid-Jan (2026: 14 Jan); Nintendo Museum and many museums closed Tue 5 Jan is *not* the case this year for Ghibli (open Tue 5 Jan) but regular Monday/Tuesday closures resume from 4–5 Jan.

## 2. General rules (with sources)

### 2.1 Restaurants and shops — the shutdown rhythm
- **Core shutdown: Tue 29 Dec 2026 – Sun 3 Jan 2027** (government/banks: 29 Dec–3 Jan by statute). Independent restaurants (ramen, izakaya, teishoku, sushi counters, neighbourhood cafés) mostly close **31 Dec or 1 Jan through 3 Jan**, many until Mon 4 or Tue 5 Jan. Markets (Tsukiji outer, Toyosu, Nishiki, Kuromon, Ōmichō, Nijō) peak 28–30 Dec, half-day on 31 Dec, nearly dead 1 Jan, trickle back 2–4 Jan, full from **Tue 5 Jan** (Tokyo wholesale 初市 5 Jan 2027). Sources: watabi.org NY guide; hiddenjapan-gems.com; shijou.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/calendar/2027.
- **1 Jan is the only truly "dead" day.** Even then: convenience stores, Don Quijote, big electronics stores (hatsu-uri from 1 Jan), station malls (Solamachi, LaLaport, Canal City, Hakata/Kyoto/Osaka station buildings), hotel restaurants, and national chains (McDonald's, KFC, Yoshinoya, Matsuya, Sukiya, Ichiran, Saizeriya, Gusto, Coco Ichi, Sushiro/Kura — branch-dependent) are open. Google Maps "holiday hours" become reliable only in the last ~10 days of December.
- **2 Jan is "opening day" for Japan's retail year**: Mitsukoshi-Isetan, Seibu-Sogo, Parco, Daimaru-Matsuzakaya, Tokyu, Odakyu, Keio, Marui, most Aeon/Ito-Yokado and outlets open 2 Jan with fukubukuro queues from ~08:00. **Takashimaya (all stores), Hankyu/Hanshin Umeda, Matsuya Ginza, GINZA SIX open 3 Jan** (Takashimaya 1–2 Jan closure is now company policy, confirmed for 2027). Sources: nikkei (主要百貨店、元日休業へ); wwdjapan; Shinjuku Takashimaya X; ginza6.tokyo news.
- **Supermarkets**: Life closed 1–3 Jan (Kansai/Tokyo, 2026); Aeon/Ito-Yokado/Seiyu/OK open most days with short hours; fresh-fish/sushi counters close. Buy fresh food by 30 Dec. (watabi.org)
- **Rural areas and onsen towns**: shops around onsen streets often close 1 Jan but ryokan serve all meals; Kinosaki, Kurokawa, Yufuin, Ginzan are *fully booked and lively* but day-use baths restricted. Plan to be in a ryokan on half-board 31 Dec–1 Jan.

### 2.3 Banks, ATMs, cash
- **Bank branches closed 31 Dec – 3 Jan** (Banking Act Enforcement Order art. 5) — i.e., last counter day Wed 30 Dec, reopen Mon 4 Jan. Currency-exchange counters at airports/big stations stay open. (watabi.org; sbishinseibank notice)
- **Seven Bank ATMs (7-Eleven)**: 24 h, 365 days, foreign Visa/Plus, Mastercard/Maestro/Cirrus, UnionPay, Amex, Discover accepted — **not JCB**. Lawson Bank and E-net (FamilyMart) ATMs also work but some pause for maintenance around midnight 31 Dec. (sevenbank.co.jp; watabi.org)
- **Japan Post Bank ATMs**: ~14,000 sites run 1–4 Jan but with holiday hours (typically 07:00–21:00 on 31 Dec and 1–3 Jan; many go offline 21:00 31 Dec → 07:00 1 Jan for system update). International cards accepted. (jp-bank.japanpost.jp; watabi.org)
- Practical: withdraw by **30 Dec**; carry ¥30–50k cash for shrines (cash-only stalls, omamori), rural buses, small onsen; IC cards top up at any station/konbini.

### 2.4 Takkyubin / luggage forwarding over NY
- Yamato (Kuroneko) and Sagawa **collect and deliver every day including 1 Jan**, but: (a) 27 Dec–4 Jan many branch counters shut or shorten hours (2025/26: >20 % of counters closed 1 Jan); (b) carriers warn of **delays of a day or more** because of holiday traffic and volume (26 Dec–5 Jan); (c) same-day/next-morning airport counters may suspend. Hotel front desks and konbini (7-Eleven/FamilyMart for Yamato) still accept parcels. **Allow 2 days (not 1) for hotel-to-hotel forwarding between 29 Dec and 4 Jan, and 3 days for Hokkaidō/Kyūshū/airport deliveries; send airport-bound bags by 1 Jan for a 4–5 Jan flight.** (kuronekoyamato.co.jp 年末年始 notices; ecnomikata.com; netshop.impress.co.jp 24 Dec 2025)

### 2.5 Medical care 29 Dec – 3 Jan (and a pregnant traveller)
- **Ordinary clinics and hospital outpatient departments close 29 Dec – 3 Jan** (often 4 Jan too). Hospital **emergency departments run 24/7** but are crowded with flu/norovirus cases (peak season); expect long waits and triage. Each city publishes a 休日当番医 / 休日急患センター roster (holiday duty clinics; Tokyo: 休日・夜間診療; Osaka: 休日急病診療所) — hotel staff can look it up; most are Japanese-only.
- **Emergency: 119** (ambulance, free; English interpreter via three-way call in major cities). **#7119** (24 h nurse/doctor triage: Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto city, Nara, Fukuoka, Sapporo, Yokohama, Saitama, Chiba etc. — not all prefectures; in Kanagawa/Hyogo/Ishikawa coverage varies) — Japanese-first, some English. **#8000** = paediatric advice line (evenings/holidays) for the kids.
- **Japan Visitor Hotline (JNTO) 050-3816-2787, 24 h/365, English/Chinese/Korean** — will locate an open English-capable hospital in your location and can interpret. This is the one number to save. (japan.travel/en/plan/hotline)
- **AMDA International Medical Information Center** (03-6233-9266, multilingual) historically **does not operate on holidays or 29 Dec – 3 Jan**; one source states the centre closed in 2024 — do **not** rely on it over NY. **Tokyo "Himawari" medical info 03-5285-8181 (09:00–20:00 daily, EN/ZH/KO/TH/ES)** does run through the holidays. (tokyo-tsunagari.or.jp; watabi.org)
- **Pregnant visitor (2nd trimester typical for travel):** before departure get an English letter from her OB (gestational age, due date, blood type, Rh, any risks, meds) and travel insurance that explicitly covers pregnancy complications; in Japan note that **obstetric emergencies go to hospital ERs with an on-call OB (当直産婦人科)** — JMIP-certified / foreign-patient hospitals with 24-h OB: Tokyo: St Luke's International (Tsukiji, 03-5550-7120, English), Tokyo Metropolitan Hiroo Hospital, Saiseikai Central (Mita), Jikei University Hospital Maternal & Child Center (24-h OB on call incl. 29 Dec–3 Jan); Kyoto: Kyoto University Hospital / Kyoto Prefectural University Hospital; Osaka: Osaka University Hospital, Osaka Police Hospital, Osaka Women's & Children's Hospital; Fukuoka: Kyushu University Hospital, Fukuoka Red Cross; Kanazawa: Kanazawa University Hospital; Hiroshima: Hiroshima University Hospital; Nagasaki University Hospital; Sapporo: Hokkaido University Hospital. **Call the Visitor Hotline first; they phone the hospital for you.** Avoid Yamadera (1,015 icy steps), Jigokudani in storm, long ropeway queues in cold; keep onsen soaks short (<10–15 min, ≤40 °C) and hydrate — Japanese OBs generally allow onsen in uncomplicated pregnancy but many ryokan baths post a caution sign. (jaog.or.jp foreign-patient guidance; hokeniryo.metro.tokyo.lg.jp hospital lists; hosp.jikei.ac.jp/boshiiryou/emergency)
- **Pharmacies:** drugstore *shop floors* (Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Welcia, Sundrug, Cocokara, Tsuruha) open most days incl. 1 Jan (shortened, some 1 Jan closures), but **prescription-dispensing counters close 29/31 Dec – 3/5 Jan**; prefectural "holiday duty pharmacies" exist near duty clinics. Bring enough prescription meds for the whole trip plus a week; OTC paracetamol (Tylenol), electrolyte drinks, antacids and pregnancy-safe basics are easy to find at drugstores/konbini. (kokodetomoru drugstore guide 2026; axu.hatenablog Matsukiyo guide)

### 2.6 Post offices
- Main counters **closed 31 Dec – 3 Jan** (last day Wed 30 Dec; reopen Mon 4 Jan; branch-size dependent). Big central post offices' **ゆうゆう窓口 (after-hours counter) stay open 31 Dec–3 Jan with reduced hours** (e.g., Tokyo Central, Shinjuku, Osaka Central, Kyoto Central) — useful for stamps/parcel pickup. Ordinary mail is not delivered on 2 Jan (and weekends); Yu-Pack/EMS/registered delivered daily. ATMs as per 2.3. (japanpost.jp 年末年始; jpnculture.net 2026-2027 guide)

### 2.7 Museums, gardens, castles — the "29 Dec – 3 Jan" rule
- **National/public museums and municipal castles/gardens**: default closure **29 Dec – 3 Jan**, with four common variants: (1) **26/28 Dec – 1 Jan, open 2 Jan** (Tokyo NM, Kyoto NM, Nara NM, NMWA, Osaka Castle, 21st Century Museum); (2) **28/29 Dec – 3 Jan** (Shinjuku Gyoen, Imperial Palace East Gardens, Kyoto Bunpaku, most prefectural museums, Nishijin Textile Center); (3) **closed only 29–31 Dec, open 1 Jan with free/ceremonial entry** (Matsumoto Castle special 1–3 Jan, Himeji 29–30 Dec only, Kumamoto Castle, Nijō Castle gardens, Hiroshima Peace Museum 30–31 Dec); (4) **private/entertainment museums open every day** (Mori, teamLab ×2, Hakone Open-Air, Ōhara, Kaiyukan, Sumida Aquarium, Skytree/Tower). Monday closures resume Mon 4 Jan, Tuesday ones Tue 5 Jan.
- Many museums post 2026/27 holiday notices only in late Nov–early Dec. Re-verify the C-confidence rows on **~10 Dec 2026**.

### 2.8 Trains and buses
- **Shinkansen**: JR Central runs **all Nozomi fully reserved 25 Dec 2026 – 5 Jan 2027** (announced); Hikari/Kodama and JR West Sanyō trains keep non-reserved cars but they fill. Peak outbound from Tokyo 29–30 Dec (morning), peak return 2–4 Jan (afternoon/evening, 3 Jan worst). Book at exactly 1 month ahead (10:00 JST) via SmartEX/EX or JR West online; **JR Pass users can reserve Nozomi only with the surcharge**. Luggage over 160 cm total needs an oversized-baggage seat reservation.
- **Timetables**: most JR and private operators run **weekend/holiday timetables 30 Dec – 3/4 Jan** (JR East: 30 Dec–4 Jan in 2025/26); fewer express services, earlier last trains in some regions, but core urban frequencies fine. **All-night services on 31 Dec**: JR East ran 6 Tokyo lines (Yamanote, Keihin-Tōhoku, Chūō-Sōbu local, Chūō rapid, Yokosuka, Sōbu/Narita) in 2025/26; Keiō, Keisei, Keikyū yes; **Tokyo Metro, Odakyū, Tōkyū no**; Kansai: Keihan, Kintetsu, Hanshin, Nankai (to Kōyasan line partial) run late/all-night to shrines; JR West limited. 2026/27 announcements come ~mid-Nov to mid-Dec. (media.jreast.co.jp/articles/5331; raillab.jp; tetsudo-ch.com)
- **Highway buses / rural buses**: Nōhi (Takayama–Shirakawa-gō–Kanazawa) run daily with reservations essential; many rural routes use Sunday timetables 29 Dec–3 Jan, and some community buses do not run 31 Dec–3 Jan. Expressways: heavy 29–30 Dec and 2–4 Jan.
- **Airports/limousine buses** operate normally; domestic flights full 29–30 Dec and 3–4 Jan.

### 2.9 What IS reliably open and lively (where to be 31 Dec – 3 Jan)
- **Shrines & temples** — the heart of the season. Best for a family with a pregnant parent (avoid the crush, go early or on 3 Jan): Sensō-ji (1 Jan from midnight; Nakamise open), Meiji Jingū (early morning), Tōdai-ji (1 Jan 00:00–08:00 free face-window), Kasuga Taisha, Fushimi Inari (dawn), Zenkō-ji, Itsukushima (midnight opening, ropeway from 05:00), Dazaifu (open through), Kenrokuen (free 31 Dec–3 Jan, all-night 31 Dec), Kōyasan Okunoin (night opening).
