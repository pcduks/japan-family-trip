# Route designs, 17 October 2026

Three designers each drew up routes in different styles for the 20 Dec 2026 – 9 Jan 2027 trip. Every route was checked against the New Year calendar, the peak travel days and Fernanda's pregnancy defaults.

- `classics.json`: the Golden Route in winter, two cities done slowly, and the Kii coast.
- `snow-onsen.json`: deep snow country, the western onsen trail, and a Kyoto house with the sea.
- `creative.json`: castles and the Inland Sea, a winter food trail, Fuji and the Pacific shore, and the far north by shinkansen.
- `regions-1.json` and `regions-2.json`: area notes, lodging, stay lengths and rates for the new bases.

Each route file keeps everything the designer recorded: stays, legs with fares, New Year's Eve, a line for every day, stop notes, trade-offs, checks, sources, the ideas they rejected and the open questions. `trip-data.json` and `v2/data/*.json` hold the integrated copy that the app reads. A few lines were corrected on the way in:
- The sun sets beside Engetsu island in winter, not through its arch.
- Kiunkaku in Atami closes 26–30 Dec.
- Adventure World is open 28 Dec – 4 Jan.
- Matsue Castle opens every day.
