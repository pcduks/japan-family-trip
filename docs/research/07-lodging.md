# 07 – Lodging research: six adults, 20 Dec 2026 – 9 Jan 2027

Researched 1 Oct 2026. Nothing booked. Party = 3 couples; one woman 29–32 weeks pregnant; two older but fit parents. In/out Tokyo.

**How to read this file.** Prices are for the whole group of six per night, JPY, rounded. Ryokan in Japan price per person with two meals; I multiply by six and then apply the New Year uplift. "Verified" = read on an official site, Ikyu, Rakuten or a current guide this week; "typical" = sector pattern where the live calendar would not render through the fetcher (Booking.com, Jalan, Airbnb and the Kurokawa association page all blocked automated reads). Live availability for a specific property on 31 Dec must be re-checked by a human in a browser; what I can give reliably is the pattern, the booking window, the terms and the odds.

**The single most important structural fact:** most mid-size and small ryokan load inventory ~6 months out (≈ 1 July 2026 for 31 Dec), and New Year (Oshōgatsu) plans post on Jalan/Rakuten/Ikyu in September–October and the best-known houses clear within weeks. On 1 October we are *inside* that window: 31 Dec at famous inns is largely gone, 29–30 Dec and 2–3 Jan are tight-but-possible, and city hotels / apartment hotels / machiya are still broadly open. Hotel-style properties (Mimaru, Tokyu Stay, Hyatt House, Dormy Inn, Kanaya, Suginoi, Taikanso) do not have the 6-month cliff and typically still show rooms in October, at NY rates.

Sources (general pattern): japanryokanguide 6-month rule and NY guide (https://www.japanryokanguide.com/en/blog/when-to-book-ryokan-japan, https://www.japanryokanguide.com/en/blog/best-ryokan-for-new-year-japan); Ryokan Finder cost guide (https://ryokanfinder.com/blog/ryokan-cost-guide-2026); Watabi NY opening guide 2026-27 (https://watabi.org/en/articles/japan-new-year-opening-guide).

## 0. Ground rules that apply everywhere

| Topic | What to expect |

Only verified figure: Hakone Ginyu +¥14,300/person. City hotels: +30–80% on 31 Dec, less on 29–30 Dec and 2–3 Jan. Apartment hotels (Mimaru etc.): dynamic, roughly 1.5–2× base. |
| **Minimum stay** | Ryokan NY plans frequently 2 nights (31 Dec + 1 Jan) and some famous houses 3. Machiya and city hotels: usually 1–2 nights. |
| **Per-person pricing** | Ryokan quote per person. For six you need 2 rooms (3+3) or 3 rooms (2+2+2). Many Japanese rooms sleep 1–6 on futon, but one room for six adults including a pregnant woman and two older parents is not advisable. |

For the pregnant traveller and parents, favour "Western-Japanese" (和洋室) rooms: beds plus a tatami area. |

## 1. Base-by-base

Legend for the NY outlook (29 Dec–3 Jan, as of 1 Oct): **OPEN** = you will find something good for six; **TIGHT** = named houses mostly gone, alternatives exist, book this week; **GONE** = effectively sold out for six at the quality tier; treat as day trip or shift dates.

### Shirakawa-gō

Fit for six: **no single gasshō house takes six in one room**; ~12–20 gasshō minshuku, typically 4–6 rooms sleeping up to ~12 total (Magoemon had 6 rooms/12 guests but its lodging has closed and it is now a restaurant). Two or three rooms at one house is possible for a group of six only by direct booking via the Tourist Association (+81-576-96-1013, https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/stay/). Shared bathrooms, steep stairs, futon, irori dinner, no elevator – a hard fit for 30+ weeks pregnant and older parents. NY outlook: **GONE / NOT ADVISABLE** – village inns close or are family-only over 31 Dec–3 Jan, buses thin out, and the January light-up nights (11, 17, 24, 31 Jan 2027 per japan-guide; Snow Monkey Resorts lists 12, 18, 25 Jan and 1 Feb – confirm on the official site) are balloted: lodging lottery applications closed **13:00 on 1 October 2026** with results 10 November. Those dates are after the trip anyway. Treat Shirakawa-gō as a day trip from Takayama or Kanazawa (bus ~50–75 min) on 4–7 Jan. Sources: https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5956.html , https://shirakawagovillage.com/ , https://www.snowmonkeyresorts.com/news/shirakawago-in-january/ .

## 2. Booking strategy for the next two weeks (1–15 Oct 2026)

1. **Decide the NY anchor first, then build outward.** The three NY nights (31 Dec–2 Jan) are the only scarce resource; everything else is still open. Choose one of: (a) a big-city apartment base (Tokyo or Kyoto/Osaka) for 30 Dec–3 Jan with a one-night onsen splurge on 29/30 Dec or 3/4 Jan; (b) a large, elevator-equipped onsen hotel that still has NY family rooms (Suginoi Beppu, Taikansō Matsushima, Takamiya Zaō, Konansou Kawaguchiko, Shogetsutei Kinosaki, Biyunoyado Yudanaka, Hakone Kowakien Ten-yu); or (c) a machiya in Kyoto/Kanazawa/Takayama for 30 Dec–3 Jan with groceries bought on the 30th. Option (b) is the one that still closes a door this week.

2. **Refundable-first, in this order.** Day 1–2: hold Tokyo arrival nights (20–22 Dec) and departure nights (7–9 Jan) on Booking.com/official sites with free cancellation (Mimaru, Tokyu Stay, Hyatt House). Day 1–3: book the NY anchor on the strictest-but-still-refundable plan you can find (official site or Ikyu/Rakuten plan with "キャンセル無料" to 14–21 days). Day 3–7: lock the second onsen stop (28–29 Dec or 3–5 Jan) at a house that fits the elevator/bed brief. Day 7–14: fill cities. Cancel duplicates as the route firms; never hold more than two options per night and clear duplicates before any 21-day cliff.

3. **Use the right channel per property type.**
   - Hotels/apartment hotels: official site or Booking.com (free cancel to 3–7 days).
   - Ryokan: Ikyu (English, shows NY plans and cancellation), Rakuten Travel English (≈10% cheaper than Booking, plans with meals), Jalan (Japanese; most listings) – use Chrome translate. Booking.com for the same ryokan often shows only room-only or a single plan.
   - Named ryokan that show "sold out" online: email the inn directly in simple English/Japanese for 2 rooms × 3 pax; allotments held back from OTAs often exist, and the 14-day cancellation cliff (mid-December for 31 Dec) releases rooms. Agencies (Japanese Guest Houses, selected-ryokan, Relux, Ryokan Collection) can lodge a two-room request and will tell you within 1–3 days.
   - Machiya: official brand sites (Machiya Residence Inn, Rinn, Nazuna, VMG/Kazeno Heritage) – paid in full at booking, refundable to 22 days (MRI) or 21 days (Nazuna).

4. **How to ask a ryokan for a group of six.** Request "2 rooms × 3 persons" or "3 rooms × 2 persons" (never "1 room × 6"), same plan, dinner at the same sitting, state one guest is pregnant (妊娠中) and two are seniors: ask for rooms near the lift, Western beds or 和洋室, low-sodium/no-raw-fish substitutions for the pregnant traveller (ryokan handle this routinely with 3+ days notice), and in-room dining if offered. Ask whether the NY plan is mandatory and its minimum nights.

5. **Deposits and payment.** Expect: official-site ryokan NY plans sometimes require card guarantee or bank transfer (Japanese bank, no fee-free route from abroad: use Wise). Ikyu/Rakuten: pay at property or card. Machiya: 100% at booking. Chalets (Hakuba/Nozawa/Niseko): 20–50% deposit on booking, balance 60 days out, non-refundable inside 60 days – buy travel insurance that covers pregnancy-related cancellation before paying these.

6. **Luggage.** Travel with cabin bags for the onsen nights and send the big cases ahead hotel-to-hotel by Yamato takkyubin (¥2,000–2,800/case, next day; +1 day 28 Dec–4 Jan). Machiya Residence Inn accepts forwarded luggage at its Welcome Desk and delivers to the house (¥500/item). Ask every hotel "荷物を事前に送ってもよいですか" when confirming.

7. **Insurance and the pregnancy.** Most airlines allow travel to 36 weeks with a letter after 28; the return (9 Jan) is at ~32 weeks. Book refundable where possible and buy a policy that covers pregnancy complications and cancellation; keep the NY ryokan the only non-refundable item.
