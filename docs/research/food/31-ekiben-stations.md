# 31 — Ekiben and station food (research note)

Feeds `data/food-guide/topics/ekiben.json` (Portuguese). Researched Oct 2026. Prices and hours are marked "verified" only when an official or Japanese primary source confirmed them; everything else in the JSON is "a confirmar".

## Key verified facts
- **Origin**: 16 Jul 1885, Utsunomiya, Shirakiya (two onigiri + takuan), now 駅弁記念日. Competing claims (Umeda/Kobe ~1877, Kumagaya/Ueno 1883, Tsuruga/Takasaki 1884). Boxed makunouchi credited to Maneki, Himeji, 1889/90. Fewer than 10 stations had platform vendors by 2016. Keiō Shinjuku ekiben fair since 1966. Self-heating (quicklime + water, pull-cord) by Awajiya, 1988. Source: ja.wikipedia 駅弁.
- **駅弁屋 祭 (GranSta Tokyo, inside gates, Central Passage)**: 5:30–22:00 daily, no fixed closing day, 150+ varieties (JR East media, info as of Feb 2026). 駅弁屋 踊 next door 6:00–21:00 sells its top 10. Sales ranking Jan–Jun 2024: 牛肉どまん中, チキン弁当, 炭火焼風牛たん弁当, まぐろいくら弁当, 牛すきと牛焼肉弁当, 深川めし, えんがわ押し寿司, 仙台名物牛たん弁当 (self-heating), とりめし, えび千両ちらし. **New Year hours were not published; marked a confirmar.**
- **駅弁味の陣 (JR East passenger vote)**: 2025 大将軍 峠の釜めし (Ogino-ya); 副将軍 山形牛すきやき牛肉重 (Matsukawa); 2024 大将軍 牛肉どまん中 塩; 2023 大館 鶏めし; Hanazen 鶏めし also won 2015 and 2016.
- **Prices verified on makers' sites**: 牛肉どまん中 ¥1,620 (Shinkineya); シウマイ弁当 ¥1,180 (Kiyōken, since 1954). Getnavi (Feb 2025): チキン弁当 ¥900, えんがわ押し寿司 ¥1,480. Prices for 峠の釜めし, ますのすし, ひっぱりだこ飯, かにめし, 鶏めし and かしわめし were not found on official pages (a confirmar).
- **GranSta Tokyo**: bento/sweets Mon–Sat 8–22, Sun 8–21. **GranSta Yaesu**: outside gates, restaurants 11–22. **ecute Shinagawa** sweets 8–22. **ecute Ueno**: inside gates 3F, bento 9–21. **Kyoto Ramen Kōji**: 10F, 11–22 (LO 21:30), 10 shops. **Eki Marché Shin-Osaka**: inside conventional-line gates (2015).
- **Himeji えきそば**: since 19 Oct 1949, kansui Chinese noodles in Japanese dashi; ten-ekisoba ¥480 (Dec 2024, secondary). **常盤軒 Shinagawa**: platform soba since 1964 (company at the station since 1922); 6:00–23:30 (2021 article).
- **Omiyage**: 東京ばな奈 (Grapestone, 1991); 萩の月 (Kashō Sanzen, 1979).

## Dropped (could not verify)
搾菜弁当, 八角弁当, 京都 萩乃家, 金華さば寿司, a named 小倉 ekiben, and the exact "sell-outs before 11:00" claim (sources only say to buy early). The 厚切り牛たん弁当 name is unconfirmed, so the JSON uses the generic self-heating gyūtan.

## Pregnancy flags
OK (fully cooked): Domannaka, Shūmai Bentō, Kamameshi, Fukagawa-meshi, Torimeshi, Kashiwa-meshi, Kanimeshi, Hipparidako, gyūtan. Caution: ますのすし, 柿の葉寿司, えんがわ (cured or marinated raw fish); Acchicchi sukiyaki (pasteurised raw egg dip). Avoid: まぐろいくら.
