## Not verified or caveats
- **「コンビニジャーナル」:** no fetchable chain-comparison article found. Chain reputations rest on LINE Research, MyVoice, Business Journal (oyakodon test 2023: Lawson tastiest, ¥635) and the onigiri survey instead.
- **7-Eleven onigiri page:** listed only three Kantō items, so smoked-salmon onigiri could not be checked. The JSON says konbini salmon onigiri is grilled (焼鮭), which is the standard, and flags smoked salmon only for sandwiches and wraps.
- **Konbini New Year opening:** no 2025/26 official statement found. Most stores open on 1 Jan, with some closures (2020 reporting). Marked "confirm your store".
- **From general knowledge, not fetched:** ministry of environment onsen-pregnancy change (2014), Maternity Mark distribution, Famiport retirement, the carrier split (Lawson and Ministop = Yu-Pack). These should be spot-checked.
- **Booking and cancellation norms and pregnancy phrases:** taken from `12-method-japanese-sources.md` §3 and §5.
- **Trusted-foodie trace:** Kenji López-Alt's konbini taste test, [YouTube 19 Aug 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TO2pblb_OzA) (from `11-critics-international.md`).
