General pregnancy notes for these regions:
- Boiled crab (zuwai, kōbako with its uchiko/sotoko roe and miso) is cooked, so it is fine. Avoid kani-sashi (raw crab) at ryokan dinners.
- Buri sashimi and buri-shabu are fine. Raw fish is OK per the brief.
- Avoid raw shiroebi (white shrimp) sashimi and raw amaebi/gasuebi. They count as raw shellfish. Order shiroebi kakiage or tempura instead.
- Avoid shirako (milt), even though it is often served as ponzu "tachi" in winter in Hokuriku.
- Avoid beef sashimi/yukke, Hida-beef nigiri (seared-rare), and the raw-egg dip for sukiyaki. Ask for the beef well cooked and skip the egg.
- Several ramen shops sell a raw egg (nama-tamago) on the side. Skip it.

## 1. Hokuriku

### Seasonal context
- **Crab:** Ishikawa's crab season opens 6 Nov. Kōbako-gani (female snow crab) is only fished until around year-end; sources say "12月いっぱい / 年末ごろ". The exact 2026 closing date is unverified; Ishikawa has historically closed on 29 Dec. **If they want kōbako, Kanazawa has to come before ~28 Dec.** Male zuwai (加能ガニ in Ishikawa, 越前がに in Fukui) runs to March, so it works in January too.
- **Kan-buri:** Himi kan-buri season is roughly Dec–Jan. It peaks exactly in the trip window.

### Ōmichō market (近江町市場)
- **New Year:** Japanese sources say it is open 31 Dec, with shops closing earlier, and **closed 1–3 Jan**, reopening around 4 Jan. That pattern comes from the 2024–25 season, via tabi-mag and banzokubiology. The 2026–27 calendar is not yet published, so confirm with 近江町市場商店街振興組合 on 076-231-1462.
- **Crowds:** 28–30 Dec is the New Year shopping rush. It is packed but fun, with crab sold by the crate.
- **Closed days:** Each stall sets its own. Check per shop.
- **Who to trust:** Tabelog's own Ōmichō roundup (matome/15760, updated Feb 2025) ranks Ikiiki-tei and Yamasan Sushi highest among the market eateries, at 3.65 each. Next come 鮨 歴々 (3.54) and 東出珈琲店 (3.61) for coffee. Most kaisen-don stalls sit at 3.40–3.49. In Tabelog terms that is ordinary, so be choosy.

### Ikiiki-tei Ōmichō (いきいき亭 近江町店)
- Ōmichō, Hokutetsu-Kanazawa · Kaisen-don, sushi · ¥2–5k
- Tabelog: 3.65 (1,088 reviews) · https://tabelog.com/ishikawa/A1701/A170101/17001392/ · Awards: none
- Japanese expert backing: Tabelog matome ranks it top of the market eateries. Reviewers praise its jimono-only (local catch only) "Local" bowl.
- Trusted-foodie trace: not checked
- Reservations: none, and it is a 10-seat counter. Write your name on the list at opening (from 7:00). Reviewers report waits of up to about 2 hours at noon. Group of 6: split 3+3 or 2+2+2 over two rotations. Go before 8:00.
- Pregnancy: a raw bowl with amaebi, gasuebi, scallop and uni. She should ask for a bowl without shellfish (fish only), or eat elsewhere.
- Dec–Jan: open 7:00–15:00, closed Tuesdays. New Year dates unverified; it most likely follows the market's 1–3 Jan closure.
- Why it's here: the one market counter that cares about the fish rather than the photo. The rice and toppings come separately so you can see each cut.

### Kanazawa oden
Kanazawa oden is eaten year-round. Kōbako appears in the "kani-men" (crab oden) only in season.

#### Oden Izakaya Miyuki (おでん居酒屋 三幸)
- Katamachi · Oden · ¥3–4k
- Tabelog: 3.63 (711 reviews) · https://tabelog.com/ishikawa/A1701/A170101/17002725/ · Awards: none
- Japanese expert backing: Tabelog matome (24332, 19489) list it with Akadama as the two canonical oden houses. It opened in 1967. Its specialities are the house satsuma-age "miyuki-age", kuruma-fu and baigai.
- Trusted-foodie trace: not checked
- Reservations: online, required for 3 or more. It has private rooms for 10–20. **Group of 6: fits.**
- Pregnancy: everything is simmered. Avoid only any raw side dishes (sashimi of shellfish).
- Dec–Jan: open 16:00–22:00, closed Sundays, public holidays and the 2nd Monday of each month. That rules out 11 Jan 2027 (Coming-of-Age Day, which is also the 2nd Monday). New Year dates unverified.
- Why it's here: the higher-scored of the two. Its dashi is lighter and cleaner, and the locals' crowd is real.

#### Akadama Honten (赤玉 本店)
