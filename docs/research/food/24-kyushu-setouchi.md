## KYUSHU

### Fukuoka: Hakata tonkotsu ramen, the critic's view

There are three schools. **Ganso Nagahamaya** is the 1952 Nagahama original: one menu item, thin noodles, a light, rough, almost brothy pork stock, and kaedama (extra noodles) culture. Locals call it "Ganso" and order it beta-nama (extra fat, barely cooked noodles). **Hakata Ikkousha** is credited with the 泡系 (foamy) style, a heavily emulsified, long-simmered "aged re-boil" broth. **Hakata Issou** was founded in 2012 by a cook trained at Ikkousha and pushed that foam further. Ramen fans nickname it 「豚骨カプチーノ」 ("tonkotsu cappuccino"), and it is the only one of the four on the current Ramen WEST 百名店 list. **Shin-Shin** is the mild, drinkable "Hakata junjō" bowl with 1 mm noodles. It is a locals' late-night choice but not a critics' pick.

Japanese sources: the Tabelog Ramen WEST 百名店 2025 Fukuoka entries are 麺道はなもこし, 八ちゃんラーメン, 魁龍 博多本店, 安全食堂, 博多元気一杯!!, 博多一双, 節ちゃんラーメン, ふくちゃんラーメン, 丸星 (Kurume), 金田家 and ぎょらん亭 (https://award.tabelog.com/hyakumeiten/ramen_west/2025). On style lineage: tabi-mag (https://tabi-mag.jp/fu0363/) and the mapple ramen guide (https://www.mapple.net/region/a0701010100_g03030000/spot/). I found no named-critic article (石神, 大崎, 北島) comparing the four shops: unverified.

### Hakata Issou, Hakata-eki Higashi honten (博多一双 博多駅東本店)
- Hakata Station east, 5 min on foot · tonkotsu ramen · ¥1,000–1,999
- Tabelog: **3.72** (5,763 reviews) · https://tabelog.com/en/fukuoka/A4001/A400101/40032701/ · Awards: **Ramen WEST 百名店 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025**
- Japanese expert backing: six straight 百名店 years. Japanese ramen writing credits its 「豚骨カプチーノ」 foam and the chef's Ikkousha training (Tabelog ratings page https://tabelog.com/fukuoka/A4001/A400101/40032701/dtlratings/).
- Trusted-foodie trace: none found
- Reservations: none, queue (budget 20–45 min at peak); 21 seats (10 counter); group of 6: split 3+3 or 2+2+2 as seats free up
- Pregnancy: fine. Skip the raw-garlic and beni-shōga toppings only if they bother her. Ajitama (marinated egg) is soft-boiled with a runny yolk, so skip it.
- Dec–Jan: open daily 11:00–24:00, closed days "not fixed". New Year: unverified.
- Why it's here: the most technically ambitious foam broth in Hakata and the critics' consensus pick. Go here if you only go once.

### Fukuoka yatai (food stalls): rules, which ones, New Year
- **Rules** (Fukuoka City Q&A, last updated 1 Apr 2022, https://www.city.fukuoka.lg.jp/keizai/yatai/charm/yataiQA.html): there are about 100 stalls in three zones (Nakasu, Tenjin, Nagahama). Prices must be displayed and "market price" (時価) is banned. Stalls must point customers to a toilet, and **raw food may not be served**. That last rule is good news for the pregnancy. City inspectors publish compliance scores. The city's 6th open call for operators (23 pitches, 102 applicants, licences running Apr 2027 – Mar 2030) is under way (city press release, Jun 2026: https://www.city.fukuoka.lg.jp/shisei/kouhou-hodo/hodo-happyo/2026/documents/260612yataieigyoukouhosyanobosyuujoukyounituite.pdf), so the current operators will still be trading in Dec 2026. Customer advice from the city: check for a seating/otōshi charge before you sit. I found no specific "2024–26 new rules" beyond this: unverified.
- **Late Dec / New Year:** no official calendar exists. Each stall sets its own holidays, and none of them are published centrally (unverified). Expect many to trade until about 30 Dec, few on 31 Dec – 3 Jan, and stalls to close in rain or strong wind anyway.
- **Stalls Tabelog users and locals respect:**
  - **Nakasu Yatai Kibun (中州屋台 紀文)**: **3.72** (114 reviews) · https://tabelog.com/en/fukuoka/A4001/A400101/40052600/ · **Izakaya WEST 百名店 2026** (and 2024). A couple has run this yakitori and oden stall for about 50 years; about ¥3,000–3,999; 19:30–23:00, closed Wed/Sun; phone reservations. ⚠ A Tabelog reviewer quotes an owner policy of **not admitting foreign guests**. Treat it as a no-go for this group.
  - **Suzaki Yatai Kajishika (須崎屋台かじしか)**: **Izakaya WEST 百名店 2026** · https://tabelog.com/fukuoka/A4001/A400106/40045563/ · score unverified. A creative-food stall away from the Nakasu tourist strip. Six people won't fit at once (yatai seat about 8–10), so go as 3+3 at different times.
- Pregnancy: raw food is banned on yatai. Check that yakitori is cooked through (no rare tsukune with raw yolk).

### Mizutaki (chicken hot pot; the best group-of-6 dinner in Fukuoka)

## Pregnancy flags (summary)
- **Avoid raw:** gomasaba (Fukuoka), basashi and horse yukke (Kumamoto), raw oysters (Miyajima), Uwajima taimeshi, the raw-egg dip at Ajimori, raw-egg kama-tama udon, and chicken sashimi at mizutaki places.
- **Judgment call:** mentaiko (eat it grilled), raw toppings on barazushi.
- **Order instead:** grilled saba, horse hot pot, kaki-furai or grilled oysters, Matsuyama-style taimeshi, shabu with ponzu, kake or zaru udon.
