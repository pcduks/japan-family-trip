Written 18 Oct 2026 from every file in this folder: `BRIEF.md`, the critic files (10, 11, 13, 14, 16, 17, 18), the method file (12), the regional files (20–24) and the topic notes (30–32). Three booking items come from `../01-events.md` and are marked as such. Nothing new was looked up for this summary. Scores are the Tabelog figures the source files read on 18 Oct 2026. "Unverified" means the source file could not confirm it.

Trip frame: six people, 20 Dec 2026 – 9 Jan 2027. Tokyo is certain for about 20–24 Dec and 5–8 Jan. The middle bases depend on the family vote. Your wife will be 29–32 weeks pregnant. 24 Dec and 31 Dec are Thursdays, 1 Jan is a Friday and 5 Jan is a Tuesday.

**The five most urgent dates:**
1. **This week (18–25 Oct):** the splurge counters that open bookings three months ahead are already taking 20 Dec – Jan, and so are Kikunoi and the Kobase crab lunch.
2. **Late October (rolling):** Otome Zushi in Kanazawa, if you want a kōbako dinner before about 28 Dec.
3. **1 Nov:** shops that release on the 1st open December and some January slots, including Sazenka's January dates.
4. **18 Nov, 17:00–19:00 JST:** Torishiki's phone window for January.
5. **1 Dec, then about 2–8 Dec:** January releases open on 1 Dec, then the Toyosu deck lottery for 6–8 Jan (two applications).

## 5. Pregnancy cheat sheet

At 29–32 weeks the hard lines are raw egg, rare meat, raw shellfish and alcohol. Cold-smoked salmon and unpasteurised cheese are Listeria risks, and bluefin is a mercury limit. Japanese OBs don't ban fresh fish nigiri, so sushi is fine if steered. **Say it at booking, never on the night**, because omakase shops buy to the headcount: 「同行者が一名妊娠中のため、生の貝類・生卵・レアのお肉は控えています。加熱したものに替えていただくことは可能でしょうか。」 At the table, use よく焼きで (well done), 生卵なしで (no raw egg) and 麹の甘酒ですか? (is it kōji amazake?). Winter adds its own traps: raw oysters, shirako ponzu, kōbako is fine but kani-sashi is not, shrine amazake, otoso and osechi smoked-salmon rolls. Chinese restaurants, tempura, unagi, tonkatsu, oden, nabe, obanzai, udon and soba are her safe havens. Logistics are the bigger risk: the 05:00 Toyosu cold, the 30 Dec Ameyoko crush and standing bars. (Files 12 §5, 20, 21 §7, 22, 23, 24.)

**Top 10 swaps**
1. **Sukiyaki raw-egg dip** (and Ajimori's kurobuta) → 生卵なしで, or shabu-shabu with ponzu.
2. **Raw oysters (生牡蠣)** at Miyajima, Tsukiji and Sendai → kaki-furai, fully grilled oysters, or oyster dote-nabe.
3. **Rare wagyu, yukke, Hida-beef nigiri, rare yakiniku or steak** → よく焼き / ウェルダン, sukiyaki cooked through, Hida beef from a steakhouse.
4. **Raw or rare chicken** (tori-sashi, rare sasami, raw-yolk tsukune, the mizutaki starter) → thigh, negima or tsukune cooked well; mizutaki once boiled.
5. **Raw shellfish at sushi** (akagai, torigai, scallop, amaebi, shiroebi, uni is a judgment call) → anago, boiled kuruma-ebi, simmered clam, tamago, kohada; shiroebi as kakiage.
6. **Shirako ponzu, tessa (fugu sashimi)** → grilled or tempura shirako, tecchiri (fugu hot pot), fried fugu.
7. **Gomasaba, basashi, Uwajima taimeshi** → grilled saba or saba-misoni, sakura-nabe (horse hot pot), Matsuyama-style taimeshi cooked in the rice.
8. **Raw-egg noodles and rice** (kama-tama, tsukimi, nikusui "tama", TKG, soft oyakodon, ajitama) → kake, zaru, kama-age; 味玉なし; ask for the egg よく火を通して.
9. **Alcohol in food:** otoso, sake-kasu amazake, kasu-jiru, Nara-zuke → kōji amazake (Ninben's dashi amazake is kōji, but ask).
10. **Bluefin toro, kinmedai, swordfish; cold-smoked salmon; raw mentaiko; ikura** → white fish, buri teriyaki or buri-shabu, grilled salmon (konbini onigiri salmon is grilled), yaki-mentaiko; a little ikura only if she's comfortable.
