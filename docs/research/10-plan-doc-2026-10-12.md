**The New Year block, three options**

- **B:** Kanazawa 4, Takayama 2, Okuhida 2. This option makes 1 Jan a 4.5–5 h travel day.

**The pace model (why each stay is this long)**
- Every activity is sized as a *main outing* (half a day) or a *short visit* (1–2 h).
- There are three paces:
  - **Gentle:** 1 main outing a day.
  - **Balanced:** 1 main outing + 1 short visit, with a 14:00–16:00 rest.
  - **Full:** 2 main outings a day.
- Nights = arrival day + full days needed for the main outings + rest days.
- Each region has its suggested nights per pace in `regions[].suggested_nights`, and the doc compares them with guidebook norms. For example, Kanazawa is 2–3 days in most guides, but the plan gives it 4. That is deliberate: it is a rest bank, and its museums close Mon 28 Dec and again from 29 Dec.

## Conflicts with 00-synthesis (Pedro to decide)

1. **New Year nights in a rural onsen town.**
   - 00-synthesis and 08-pregnancy rule 8: the 29 Dec – 3 Jan bases should be cities with a perinatal centre, not rural onsen towns.
   - The plan doc puts **30 Dec – 1 Jan in Gero**, a rural onsen town. Gero's hospital and obstetrics status was not checked.
   - Takayama has the Takayama Red Cross Hospital (08-pregnancy). Kanazawa has Kanazawa University Hospital, and Kenroku-en is open all night on 31 Dec (00-synthesis).
   - **Likely fix:** spend New Year's Eve in Kanazawa or Takayama, not Gero.
2. **Travel days.**
   - 00-synthesis (JR data): the worst train days are **27–30 Dec** and 2–4 Jan; 31 Dec and 1 Jan are calm.
   - The plan doc counted 29–31 Dec as peak. It moves on **28 Dec** (Nohi bus via Shirakawa-go) and **30 Dec** (45 min local train), and treats 31 Dec as a day to avoid.
   - **Likely fix:** move to Takayama by 26–27 Dec at the latest, or use 31 Dec / 1 Jan for any move.
3. **Pace for the parents.**
   - 00-synthesis: the parents are "older but very fit: 10 km/day is easy".
   - The plan doc treated the parents as needing a gentler pace.
   - The constraint is really hers: ≤8–10 km/day, ≤4 h of travel per day, and a rest day after any travel day of ≥3 h. That fits the Balanced pace for everyone, with split days for the fit four.
4. **5 Jan Kyoto → Hakone (3–3.5 h) followed by a full day on 6 Jan.** This brushes 08-pregnancy's "rest day after a ≥3 h travel day". Make 6 Jan a lighter day.
5. **Lodging feasibility.**
   - 07-lodging says New Year ryokan inventory is mostly gone (for example, Hakone Ginyu on 31 Dec).
   - The plan doc's Gero ryokan picks (Yunoshimakan, Suimeikan, Bosenkan) were not checked for availability for six on 30–31 Dec.
6. **Milestones.**
   - `docs/prd-v2.md` milestones assume a greenfield build.
   - The real dates are in 00-synthesis: vote closes 15 Oct, bookings locked 20 Oct.
7. **Tokyo food list.**
   - The plan doc's copy has 58 entries; `data/tokyo-foodlist.json` has 56.
   - Keep the repo's version.
