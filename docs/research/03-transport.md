# 03 — Transport & logistics: six travellers, Japan, 20 Dec 2026 – 9 Jan 2027

Party: pregnant woman (29–32 wk), two older parents, two couples in their 30s = 6 adults.
Research date: 1 Oct 2026. Where 2026/27 facts are published they are marked **[announced]**; otherwise last year's pattern is given with a confidence tag **[pattern, high/med/low]**.

## 2. Rail passes

### 2.1 Nationwide JR Pass
- Prices (from official site): 7-day ¥50,000 / Green ¥70,000; 14-day ¥80,000 / ¥110,000; 21-day ¥100,000 / ¥140,000. **From 1 Oct 2026 agent-sold prices rise ~5–6% (7d ¥53,000, 14d ¥84,000, 21d ¥105,000; Green 7d ¥74,000)** [announced; whether the official JR site mirrors the rise is reported inconsistently — check japanrailpass.net when buying].
- Nozomi/Mizuho require a surcharge voucher even with the pass; during the Nozomi all-reserved windows pass holders must still reserve.
- Break-even for a 21-day pass ≈ ¥100k of point-to-point rail. A 20-night trip with 4–6 bases typically has 5–7 inter-base legs ≈ ¥45–75k pp plus day trips ≈ ¥10–20k → **below break-even for ordinary; far below for Green (¥140k).** Only a route like Tokyo→Kanazawa→Kyoto→Hiroshima→Fukuoka→Tokyo (≈¥75k) + day trips approaches it. Verdict: **skip**, unless the route includes Fukuoka↔Tokyo by rail both ways (which R5 forbids anyway).
