# 09 — Experience catalog: build notes (1 Oct 2026)

`data/experiences.json` (158 cards), `data/bases.json` (55 bases) and `data/transit.json` (113 legs) are generated by `npx tsx scripts/import-catalog.ts` from the card tables at the end of `05-north-west.md` and `06-south-west-hokkaido.md`, plus 14 Tokyo/Kantō cards (`TK-*`) lifted from `01-events.md` / `02-closures.md` (the two region files start west of Tokyo and the deck needs a Fuji/Tokyo axis). Hand fixes live in `scripts/catalog-overrides.json` and are merged last, so re-running the script is reproducible. Every parsed field is logged to `scripts/catalog-review.csv` (id, field, raw, parsed, needs_review).

## Normalisation rules (so you can predict the output)

- **bump_ok** `"2 / 5"` → `bump_ok 2`, `bump_ok_alt 5` (alt is kept only when it is *higher*: `"3 / 1"` on Himeji means grounds vs keep for the same person, so it stays 3). `"n/a"` on ski cards → `bump_ok 1` (not for her) + `bump_ok_alt 4` (fit four). Ranges (`"3–4"`) → max.
- **dates_valid** → `valid`: `all window/any/daily` → `{}`; a span ≤3 days → `only[...]`; longer spans clamped to 20 Dec–9 Jan → `from/to`; `not …` / `museum not …` / `closed …` → `closed[...]`; `closed Wed` / `not Tue` → `weekdays`; `Saturdays` → `weekdays [6]`; `from 27 Dec` → `from`. Anything else lands in `needs_review` as `dates: …`.
- **booking_lead** → `lead_days` = max of the units found (wk ×7, mo ×30); `none` → 0; `stay/guests` → null; a date only becomes `deadline` through overrides (Nintendo 2026-10-31, Chion-in 2026-12-01, Zōjō-ji 2026-12-01, Ghibli Museum 2026-12-10).
- **suits** → `suits_default` (`all` = her+parents+bros+pedro; `fit four` = parents+bros; `five, NOT partner` = parents+bros+pedro) and `split_group` = true whenever she is excluded or the text splits the group.
- **anchor** = overnight, or hours ≥3, or uniqueness ≥4 (recomputed after overrides).
- **ny_status** (28 Dec–5 Jan): when the card fuzzy-matches a venue row in `02-closures.md` (name tokens; ties/false positives fixed through `NY_ALIASES`), the row's O/C/R cells become open/closed/reduced, `event` when the cell mentions free entry / all-night / bell / sunrise, and the row's A/B/C becomes `confidence`. Otherwise the card's own `ny_status` text is parsed (`closed 29–31 Dec`, `ends 27 Dec`, `closed NY`, `sold out` → reduced, `event`). Dates excluded by `valid` are forced to `closed`. Cards with none of that are `unknown`.
- **tags** are keyword-derived (onsen, snow, ski, food, shrine, temple, art, market, view, lights, ride, castle, garden, walk, night, ny, overnight, split) plus `cut`.
- **cut** cards (kept for completeness, builder skips `tags: cut`): KS-10 Yoshino, KY-08 Aso (crater closed), KY-11 Gunkanjima, KS-12 Tōka Ebisu, NW-NII-05 Sado, NW-FUJ-12 Shimoda, NW-KUS-07 Ikaho steps, NW-KAN-11 Noto bus day, NW-TOH-04 Yamadera, and ST-03 Inujima (06 says "cut unless art is the theme").
- **HK-*** cards are dropped (Hokkaido out); no `sapporo` base.

## Bases

`bases.json` holds the 36 requested slugs plus every other base a card references: atami, toyama, fukui (Awara/Mikuni), kobe (Arima), takamatsu, onomichi, matsuyama, yuzawa, niigata, yamagata, aizu, nyuto, morioka, towada, hachinohe, aomori, takachiho, kagoshima, yakushima. Sub-places map onto the nearest base (Kinugawa/Yumoto → nikko, Shima/Ikaho → kusatsu, Shibu → yudanaka, Myōkō → nagano, Shirahone → matsumoto, Hirayu → takayama, Takaoka → kanazawa, Minakami → yuzawa, Shuzenji/Shimoda → atami, Noto → kanazawa). Hospitals come from `08-pregnancy.md §4`; where §4 has nothing (rural onsen towns) the nearest city hospital with obstetrics is given with realistic door-to-door minutes, `perinatal` true only for university / Red Cross / designated perinatal centres. `lodging_tier`/`ny_tier` follow 07 (1 OPEN, 2 TIGHT, 3 GONE). `to_hnd_minutes` is door-to-door to Haneda (Kyushu/Hiroshima/Matsuyama by flight). `airport` uses the type's enum only (HND/KIX/FUK/ITM), so Komatsu, Hiroshima, Sendai etc. are `null`.

## The 36-card trunk deck — rationale

Chosen so that a "yes" pulls the route toward a specific shape: north/Hokuriku (Kanazawa NY block, Nagano/Nozawa, Hida, Tōhoku), Kansai (Kyoto NY, Kinosaki, Kōyasan, Nara), Setouchi (Okayama/Kurashiki, Miyajima, Takamatsu), Kyushu (Fukuoka hub, Kurokawa, Takachiho, Nagasaki, Dazaifu) and Fuji/Tokyo (Kawaguchiko, Hakone/Ekiden, Tokyo NY days). Each group has at most two cards from the same axis; generic "every city has one" items (station soba, local lunches) are deliberately not in the deck.
