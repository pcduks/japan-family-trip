**Which to trust when they disagree:** the numbered files (01–07) are the current state and are newest. `research/route-review-2026-10-17.md` is the latest reasoning on the route; it replaced the Takayama stop in `research/plan-doc/01-the-plan.md`. `research/background/` is the 1 Oct groundwork (still the source for closures, events, transport and pregnancy facts). Prices and availability for places to stay were checked on 23 Oct 2026 and change fast.

## Files

- `01-our-route.md`: The route we are taking: stops, dates, journeys, cost, checks, the reasoning and every day planned.
- `02-other-routes.md`: The ready-made changes to our route, compared with it, and the earlier whole routes we looked at before the 17 Oct review.
- `03-places.md`: Every part of Japan we looked at (42 areas): how it fits our route, what it is known for, what it is like on our dates, how long to stay, and every place to visit in it.
- `04-where-to-stay.md`: For every stop: which part of town and why, the researched places to stay (rooms, beds, lift, bath, New Year, prices, links), the enquiry emails, and the parts of town for stops on the other routes.
- `05-deadlines-budget-rules.md`: Every booking deadline, how the budget is worked out, and the rules the checks use (busy travel days, how many nights a place can fill, hospitals).
- `06-food.md`: How we eat on this trip, ryokan meals, what suits Fernanda, and the food guide for every town.
- `07-day-plans-by-place.md`: The ready-made day plans for every place (what a good day there looks like, which days it can't be done), which the routes are built from.
- `research/route-review-2026-10-17.md`: The 17 Oct review of the route that led to dropping Takayama (latest reasoning on the route).
- `research/plan-doc/` (9 files): The plan doc (12–13 Oct): the plan, why each stay is this long, what we're missing, region guides.
- `research/background/` (10 files): Background research from 1 Oct: events, closures, transport, lodging, pregnancy, regions.
- `research/food/` (18 files): Food research: critics, sources, restaurants, shops by region.
