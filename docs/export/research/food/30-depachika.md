# 30 · Depachika (department-store food halls)

Researched 18 Oct 2026 for `data/food-guide/topics/depachika.json`. Source rules follow `BRIEF.md`. Builds on `21-tokyo-shops-markets-tours.md` §1 and `02-closures.md` §2.2. The web-search budget is shared, so this pass used about 20 searches and fetches. Several official store sites returned 403 or 503 (mistore.jp, takashimaya.co.jp/nihombashi).

## Verified this session
- **History.** ja.wikipedia デパ地下: the first basement food floor was Matsuzakaya Nagoya in 1936. Hankyu pushed food because it lacked a kimono heritage. The word spread in 2000 with Tokyu Food Show. Hanshin built its "日本一のデパ地下" reputation on everyday food. Basements were chosen for cheap utilities and links to the subway and parking (the 噴水効果, or fountain effect). Hankyu Umeda opened on 15 Apr 1929 as the "world's first terminal department store", and its dining hall sold 13,000 curries a day before the war (ja.wikipedia 阪急うめだ本店). Hanshin's Snack Park dates from 1957 as the おやつセンター, and the store was rebuilt 2018–22 (ja.wikipedia 阪神百貨店).
- **Daimaru Tokyo bento ranking 2025** (Jan–Dec sales, published 2 Apr 2026, Travel Watch): 1 Takimoto ¥1,795; 2 Hirashima ¥1,380; 3 Ation ¥1,380; 4 Kakiyasu Gyūmeshi ¥1,598; 5 Seiyō Ginza ¥1,598; 6 Kameido Masumoto ¥1,458; 7 Maisen ¥1,382; 8 Kaneko Hannosuke ¥1,450; 9 Kanezaki ¥1,200; 10 Benmatsu 並6 白付 ¥1,458. The hall is ほっぺタウン, with a 60 m bento street. https://travel.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2098587.html (the 2022–23 list is at https://tetsudo-ch.com/12879294.html).
- **Kiyōken シウマイ弁当: ¥1,180** incl. tax, sold since 1954; the company was founded in 1908. https://kiyoken.com/products/obento/shiben.html
- **Asakusa Imahan**, founded 1895. Prices from the official counter page: すき焼弁当 ¥2,916–4,860, 重ねすき焼弁当 ¥2,700, 牛肉弁当 and 牛玉弁当 ¥1,674. The page lists the Nihonbashi Mitsukoshi counter, and prices were revised 1 Oct 2026. https://www.asakusaimahan.co.jp/bento/buy/. Ningyōchō Imahan is a separate company, also from 1895, with a すき焼弁当 (https://www.imahan.com/).
- **Benmatsu**: the Higuchiya eatery opened in 1810, and the bento-only Benmatsu followed in 1850. Its own slogan is "濃ゆい味、江戸日本橋の味". (ja.wikipedia 日本橋弁松総本店; benmatsu.com)
- **551 Hōrai 豚まん**: 2 for ¥500, 4 for ¥1,000, 6 for ¥1,500. https://www.551horai.co.jp/menu/butaman/. It is listed under the Matsuko シュウマイ theme in a Tabelog matome (https://tabelog.com/matome/5355/), a user-made, unofficial list.
- **Hanshin ikayaki**: B1 Snack Park, up to 10,000 sold a day (Daily Sports, 3 Jul 2026). Mapple lists いか焼き ¥187, デラバン ¥242 and 和風デラ ¥253, but those prices are **undated, so a confirmar**. Store hours are 10:00–20:00 and Snack Park 10:00–22:00 (hanshin-dept.jp).
- **Kūya**: one Ginza shop only, with no mail order. Phone reservation is the norm (03-3571-3304, reported). After the Feb 2025 rise, a box of 10 costs ¥1,200 (plain) or ¥1,300 (gift box), per pricey.jp and co-trip. Tabelog 3.80 (2,294 reviews), per file 21.
- **Funasa**: founded 文久2 (1862), at 日本橋室町1-12-13. TV Tokyo calls it the originator of tsukudani.
- **Others**: Ginza Wakana was established in 1956; its 真鯛のミルフィーユ won the MAFF minister's prize at the 漬物グランプリ 2022, and reviews place it on Ginza Mitsukoshi B2 (atpress). Minokichi celebrates its 310th year in 2026, which implies 1716. Tori Sanwa dates from 1900 (Nagoya Cochin). Toraya was founded in Kyoto in the late Muromachi period and has a 虎屋菓寮 at Isetan Shinjuku. RF1 is run by Rock Field and releases new items on the first Thursday of each month.
- **New Year**: in 2025 Hankyu Honten, Takashimaya and Daimaru Matsuzakaya moved 初売り to 3 Jan (WWD Japan), and Hankyu opened 30 min early because of the queue. For 2027, `02-closures.md` has Takashimaya closed 1–2 Jan (company-confirmed) and Mitsukoshi-Isetan opening 2 Jan at 10:00 (grade B). Other stores are grade C.
