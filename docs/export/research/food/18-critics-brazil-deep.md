# Critic deep-dive: the Brazilian-list accounts (Ian Oliver, Danilo Nakamura, Eduardo Takemi, The Hungry Tourist, Laercio Munechika)

Research date: 18 Oct 2026. This builds on `10-critics-brazil.md`; identities are not re-argued here. Brief: `CRITIC-DIVE-BRIEF.md`. Instagram itself was not read.

**Headline findings**
- **Ian Oliver (@ocritico.antigourmet) has been to Japan and reviewed it.** His own site, ocriticoantigourmet.com.br, has a scored head-to-head of **Sushi Saito (9.5/10) vs Sawada (10/10)**, a demolition of **Izakaya Toyo** in Osaka (3/10), and a pan of the **Sukiya** gyudon chain. He also sells a sold-out **"Japão 2027"** group tour (8–26 Jan 2027) whose itinerary names **Sushi Sanshin** (Osaka), **Bar Rocking Chair** (Kyoto), **Sano miso**, **Imadeya / Hasegawa Saketen** sake shops and **Tsukiji Sushi Sei**. The earlier profile said "no Japan found"; that is now wrong.
- **The Hungry Tourist's 2027 "Grand Japan" tour pages** give Dudi Califa's own words on Sugita, Saito, Arai, Sora, Motoyoshi, Shima, Kyoto's Maeda and Tempura Matsu. All Tokyo/Kyoto core places were Tabelog-checked (below).
- **Eduardo Takemi**: still only one Japan piece (Kurokawa, Dec 2025), but it adds that he has "ido várias vezes ao Japão" and gives concrete onsen tips.
- **Danilo Nakamura**: no Japan trip found. His full LeFigado blog (26 posts) and his 2008–10 Food Hunter blog were read end to end. His sake palate and his São Paulo Japanese/drinks picks are documented.
- **Laercio Munechika**: nothing new. The only trace is the São Paulo lawyer directory entry ([Escavador](https://www.escavador.com/sobre/4425328/laercio-tadashi-munechika)). Searches on "宗近", Nikkei, photography and Japan found nothing.

**Japan places found: 54** (Ian Oliver 11, Eduardo Takemi 1, The Hungry Tourist 42, Danilo Nakamura 0). That counts named restaurants, bars, shops, markets and lodgings, and leaves out tour slots described without a name. Tabelog detail pages were fetched for 26 of them; award and 百名店 data cover a few more.

## 1. Ian Oliver, "O Crítico Antigourmet"

### 1.1 Who (additions to file 10)
- He runs **ocriticoantigourmet.com.br**: reviews ("Minha crítica ao…"), sensory courses (cheese, wine, olive oil), consulting, and **group food trips** (Spain, Scotland, Piedmont, Tuscany, Japan) of about 12 people that he accompanies in person ([Viagens](https://www.ocriticoantigourmet.com.br/viagens)). The home page says "Agora estamos no jornal O Globo" and pushes a free newsletter ([home](https://www.ocriticoantigourmet.com.br/)). The paid "Seja um assinante" pitch is "Ajude o perfil a não depender de publis!" ([Críticas](https://www.ocriticoantigourmet.com.br/criticas)).
- His O Globo column of 1 Oct 2026 hit **Don Julio** (Buenos Aires) for having "stopped serving food and started winning prizes". Pablo Rivero answered with an open letter ([Folha PE, 6 Oct 2026](https://www.folhape.com.br/sabores/pablo-rivero-don-julio-parrilla-carta-aberta/516909/)). That shows he is willing to take on 50 Best darlings.
- He scores on a mix of scales (x/10, x/5, or words: "muito ruim", "ruim", "muito bom") and **always states the price paid**.
- He does not eat salmon: "ressalvando que não como Salmão" ([Quina do Futuro review, 13 Jan 2025](https://www.ocriticoantigourmet.com.br/minha-critica-ao-quina-do-futuro)).

### 1.2 Palate in his own words
1. On sushi rice at Saito: "O equilíbrio salgado é calculado para realçar o peixe, nunca competir com ele. Como é mantido a 38°C… o grão permaneça firme, mas jamais seco." ([Sawada VS Saito, 15 Feb 2025](https://www.ocriticoantigourmet.com.br/sawada-vs-saito))
2. His test for sushi is the bolus: "o que mais importa no sushi é a formação do bolus: como o arroz e peixe se transformam em um alimento único na mastigação." (same)
3. On Sawada's no-photo rule: "Confesso que inicialmente me incomodou… No fim da refeição… entendi por quê. Prestei atenção a elementos da comida que não teria prestado se estivesse preocupado em tirar fotos." (same)
4. On hype street food: "Quando se tira o filtro do Instagram e se senta, o que resta é um sushi tecnicamente medíocre… O Toyo prova que storytelling barato não é exclusividade de restaurante chique." ([Izakaya Toyo, 6 Apr 2025](https://www.ocriticoantigourmet.com.br/minha-critica-ao-izakaya-toyo))
5. The benchmark for real street food is Jay Fai: "comida de rua de verdade: com sabor, com técnica, com verdade." (same)
6. On toppings as insecurity: "Molho de pimenta, maçarico, o abominável azeite trufado. Adicionar essa quantidade de coisa só reflete a insegurança do local… O arroz, que deveria ser o foco, é horrível: passado, empapado, blocado e muito doce." ([Quina do Futuro, 13 Jan 2025](https://www.ocriticoantigourmet.com.br/minha-critica-ao-quina-do-futuro))
7. On fast food: "Particularmente não espero nada de um fast-food além de correção e padrão." ([Sukiya, 30 Nov 2024](https://www.ocriticoantigourmet.com.br/minha-critica-ao-sukiya))
8. What he praises: "não tenta te impressionar com apetrechos e penduricalhos, mas com ponto, execução e busca obsessiva pelo melhor ingrediente." ([Cepa, 6 Apr 2025](https://www.ocriticoantigourmet.com.br/minha-critica-ao-cepa))
9. On awards: "Premiações são uma cilada. Erguem um pedestal tão alto que a queda se torna inevitável." ([Casa do Porco, 31 Jan 2025](https://www.ocriticoantigourmet.com.br/minha-critica-a-casa-do-porco))
10. On aging fish, a backhanded note in the Saito/Sawada piece: "peixes perfeitamente maturados (lá a maturação funciona)". He is sceptical of fish aging in general and grants it only where it is executed well.

### 1.5 Japan tips (his)
- **Booking Sawada:** "A reserva é muito difícil: elas abrem a conta-gotas em um site, sem avisar o momento em que abrirão… visitei a página umas 5 ou 6 vezes. Dei sorte." Refresh the booking page repeatedly ([source](https://www.ocriticoantigourmet.com.br/sawada-vs-saito)).
- **No photos at Sawada**, and he came to see the point of the rule (quote above). Sawada "Não fala inglês".
- **Netflix/Chef's Table fame ruins places:** Toyo became a queue to "comer em pé" at tourist prices.
- **Mix high and low:** his own tour pairs Michelin counters with tonkatsu, onigiri, takoyaki, depachika-style sweets, sake shops and markets.

### 1.7 Persona card: Ian Oliver
Judges the plate, never the room, the queue or the star. He asks: what temperature is the shari? Does the bolus come together? Is the doneness exact? Is anything there to hide the product (blowtorch, truffle oil, sweet sauces, "export" tuna)? He always states the price and asks whether the plate is worth it. **Veto:** Netflix-famous street food with queues; blowtorch "signatures"; Brazilian-style toppings; salmon (he doesn't eat it). **Loves:** austere Edomae, and canonical classics executed correctly at any price level. *Sample verdict:* "Tirando o filtro do Instagram, sobra um arroz passado e um peixe semicongelado. Nota 3." **To this family:** "Grávida não come cru? Ótimo teste: tempurá, unagi, tonkatsu e yakitori bem passados mostram a técnica de um cozinheiro melhor que qualquer otoro. Pulem o Toyo; comam enguia."

**Confidence:** High. These are his own signed pages, with dates and prices. The exact dates of his Japan trip are unverified, and the unnamed tour slots are unknown.

### 2.7 Persona card: Danilo Nakamura
A drinker first, funny and fair. He asks: what is in the glass, and does the house know its sake, shochu and vermouth? Is the price honest for what it is? Would I come back on a random Tuesday? He respects classics done right and cheap pleasures done honestly. He shrugs at lists: "é só uma lista". **Veto:** pretension without delivery, wrong-temperature service, gimmick martinis, tourist "experiences". *Sample verdict:* "É bom. Sólido. ### 3.2 Palate in his own words
