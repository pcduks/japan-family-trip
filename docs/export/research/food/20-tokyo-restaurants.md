## How this was checked (read first)
- **Every score below was read from the restaurant's own Tabelog page** (English mirror `tabelog.com/en/…`, same data as the Japanese page) on 18 Oct 2026. That includes the score, review count, price band, seats, private rooms, reservation policy and weekly closures. Award and 百名店 years come from the badges on the same page. Candidates were taken from the current **Tabelog 百名店** Tokyo/East lists (`award.tabelog.com/hyakumeiten/<genre>`: sushi_tokyo 2025, tempura 2025, tonkatsu 2026, unagi 2026, soba_east 2025, ramen_tokyo 2025, yakitori_east 2025, yakiniku_tokyo 2026, sukiyaki_shabushabu 2024, japanese_tokyo 2025, chinese_tokyo 2026, izakaya_east 2026, tachinomi 2025, okonomiyaki 2025, yoshoku_east 2025, kissaten 2026, wagashi_tokyo 2026, sweets_tokyo 2026, cafe_east 2025, toriryori 2025).
- **Japanese expert backing** here means the Tabelog Award (Gold/Silver/Bronze) and 百名店 selections, which are the Japanese-language editorial layer on Tabelog. **I could not check Michelin JP, dancyu, TRY or Japanese bloggers on this pass,** because the shared web-search budget for this run was used up before I reached that step. Treat those lines as "not checked", not "none".
- **Trusted-foodie traces** come from the sibling research in `10-critics-brazil.md` and `11-critics-international.md` (Mark Wiens / migrationology, We Are Hungry Tourists, Little Meg), with their links. I could not run new searches for Pete Wells, Kenji or the Brazilian accounts. "None found" means none in that research, not a proven absence.
- **Dates:** 24 Dec 2026 is a **Thursday**. 5–8 Jan 2027 is Tue–Fri. "24 Dec: open" below means the regular weekly schedule on Tabelog has the place open on Thursdays. Christmas Eve bookings will still be tight. **New Year closures are given only where Tabelog states them.** Otherwise assume ~29/30 Dec – 3/4 Jan, which is outside the family's Tokyo dates anyway. Re-check the first week of January, since some places reopen only on 5 or 6 Jan.
- **Price bands** are Tabelog's (dinner / lunch, ¥ per head).
- **Pregnancy shorthand:** at any counter, say when booking: 「妊娠中のため、生の貝類・生卵・レアのお肉は控えたいです」 ("pregnant: no raw shellfish, raw egg or rare meat"). Japanese OBs allow fresh fish nigiri. Limit bluefin (maguro/toro).

## B/C) Shortlist: 49 Tabelog-verified places

**Top 10 for this family** (quality × can-6-get-in × dates):
1. **Sushi Arai** (4.59, Gold): the one sushi splurge. It has a 6-seat private room.
2. **Mikawa Zezankyo** (4.10, ten straight Bronze Awards): tempura, with a room for 6.
3. **Tonkatsu Narikura** (4.29): open Thursdays, so 24 Dec works.
4. **Stamina-en** (4.27, Award Silver/Gold since 2017): locals queue for this yakiniku in Adachi.
5. **Uwo Toku** (3.94): its 6-seat kotatsu counter is a perfect fit for 6.
6. **Kagurazaka Ishikawa** (4.41) or **Sazenka** (4.59): pick at most one ¥50–60k night. Both have a room for 6.
7. **Obana + Maruchiba**: a Minami-Senju unagi lunch, then an izakaya.
8. **Ginza Hachigo** (3.93): the best ramen here that is bookable.
9. **Narutomi** (3.73): soba, with a table for 6. Mark Wiens has been.
10. **Yakiniku Jumbo Shirokane** (3.73): rooms for 6, The Hungry Tourist's yakiniku.

**Budget reality:** S$15k per couple over 20 nights ≈ ¥1.7M per couple for everything. Choose **one** ¥50k+ night (Arai, Ishikawa, Myōjaku or Sazenka), not several.

### Sushi

### Sushi Arai (鮨 あらい)
- Ginza · Sushi · ¥50,000–59,999 (dinner and lunch)
- Tabelog: **4.59** (1,083 reviews) · https://tabelog.com/tokyo/A1301/A130101/13188186/ · Awards: Award Gold 2017, 2020–2026; Silver 2018–19; 百名店 Sushi TOKYO 2022, 2025
- Japanese expert backing: Tabelog Award Gold for seven straight years, among the top tier of Tokyo sushi. Michelin JP and other critics not checked.
- Trusted-foodie trace: **Mark Wiens** booked the whole counter on The Hungry Tourist tour ([video, 22 Nov 2017](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cm2dczXpBG0)). **We Are Hungry Tourists** lists it as core of its "Tokyo Super Tour" ([THT](https://www.thehungrytourist.com/tours/best-of-tokyo)).
- Reservations: Tabelog takes bookings (open ~1–2 months ahead; book the moment the window opens). Seats: 7 at the counter plus a **6-seat private room (¥10,000 room fee, groups of 4+)**. Group of 6: **fits in the private room.** For the counter experience, split 3+3 over two services.
- Pregnancy: famous for its tuna. She should limit maguro/toro and skip raw shellfish (akagai, torigai, raw scallop). Uni is a judgment call. She can order kohada and other cured fish, plus cooked pieces: anago, kuruma-ebi, tamago, simmered clam. Mention it at booking.
- Dec–Jan: closed Wed. Thu dinner only, so **24 Dec: dinner open** (expect it full). New Year closure not stated.
- Why it's here: Arai Yuichi's counter is the best-scored sushi in this list that a group of 6 can enter as one party. The rice is assertive and the tuna is the point.

- Dec–Jan: closed Tue/Wed. **24 Dec: open.**
- Why it's here: the alternative if Arai is full. Two chefs keep an eight-seat room tight and precise.
