# Kanazawa and Shirakawa-go

**Thu 24 – Mon 28 Dec, 4 nights.** Do the museums on 24–27 Dec. Monday 28 Dec is a closing day for several of them, and most close again from 29 Dec. Kenroku-en and the castle park are open every day.

## Before you plan

- **[21st Century Museum](https://www.kanazawa21.jp/data_list.php?g=7&d=1)** is closed Mon 28 Dec and 29 Dec – 1 Jan. It is open 10:00–18:00, and until 20:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. The *Swimming Pool* lower level needs a [same-day timed booking from 9:00](https://www.kanazawa21.jp/data_list.php?g=120&d=1).
- **[Nomura Samurai House](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4204.html)** is closed 26–27 Dec, so go on 24 or 25 Dec. The [Visit Kanazawa year-end guide](https://visitkanazawa.jp/en/feature/detail_532.html) lists other closures.
- **[D.T. Suzuki Museum](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4211.html)** is closed Mondays and 29 Dec – 3 Jan.
- **[Kenroku-en](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4200.html)** is open 8:00–17:00 and free from 6:00 until opening, November to February. The [yukitsuri snow ropes](https://visitkanazawa.jp/en/event/detail_50425.html) will be up.
- **Crab:** the female kobako crab is in season [only in November and December](https://www.japan.travel/en/articles-from-2020/kobakogani-snow-crab-of-kaga). For her, choose the cooked crab and skip the raw roe and innards.

## Shirakawa-go on 28 Dec

Take the 8:40 Nohi bus from Kanazawa, which reaches Shirakawa-go at 10:05. Leave at 12:35 and reach Takayama at 13:25. Every departure needs a [reservation](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shirakawago_en/), and you need two separate tickets for a stopover. Booking seems to open about one month ahead ([Japan Bus Online](https://japanbusonline.com/en/CourseSearch/11900040002)). Snow can delay buses by two hours or more.

- **Luggage:** lockers and a staffed baggage service at the bus terminal, open 8:00–17:00 ([official FAQ](https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/en/faq/5/)). Better still, forward the big bags straight to Takayama.
- **Viewpoint:** take the shuttle from Wada House (every 20 min, 10:00–14:40). The walking trail closes in snow.
- **Houses open inside:** [Wada House](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5951.html) 9:00–17:00. The houses have ladder-like stairs, so she can stay on the ground floor.
- **Ice:** waterproof boots for everyone, and slip-on spikes for her ([December conditions](https://www.snowmonkeyresorts.com/news/shirakawago-in-december/)). The 2027 winter light-up falls on 11, 17, 24 and 31 Jan, after you pass through.
- Links: [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Shirakawa-go+Ogimachi) · [Photos](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Shirakawago+winter) · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/shirakawago/) · [Video: walking tour in heavy snow](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t3rGu4cPqSk)

## Food

For her, the cooked dishes are oden, jibuni (a duck stew), tempura, katsu curry and cooked crab. Ask for *nama-mono nashi* (nothing raw).
