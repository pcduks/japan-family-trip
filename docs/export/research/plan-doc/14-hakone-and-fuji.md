# Hakone and Fuji

> **Changed on 16 Oct 2026: Kawaguchiko is now the route's Fuji stop, 5–7 Jan, at a lake hotel with beds and a lift; Hakone is the alternative.** Pedro and Fernanda have done Hakone, and the lake has the straight-on Fuji view and is new for everyone. The comparison below still holds; only the choice changed.

**Tue 5 – Thu 7 Jan, 2 nights. Hakone is recommended over Kawaguchiko.** It is 1–2 hours closer to Kyoto with one main change, 80 minutes from Tokyo, and has five good museums for a cloudy day. January is the best month for Fuji either way.

## Hakone or Kawaguchiko

|  | Hakone | Kawaguchiko |
| --- | --- | --- |
| From Kyoto on 5 Jan | About 3–3.5 h door to door: Hikari to Odawara (about 2h05; Nozomi doesn't stop), then the mountain railway ([japantrain.net](https://japantrain.net/guide/kyoto-odawara-shinkansen), [japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5202.html)) | About 4–4.5 h via Mishima and a 1h30 bus ([fujilake.co.jp](https://www.fujilake.co.jp/english/access.html)), or 5+ h via Tokyo |
| To Tokyo on 7 Jan | About 80 min by Romancecar | About 2 h by Fuji Excursion train or 1h45 by bus |
| Fuji views | From the lake shore, the boats, the ropeway and the Narukawa museum lounge ([japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e5201.html)); ridges can hold cloud | Straight on across the lake; the postcard view |
| On a cloudy day | Five art museums and onsen | Fewer indoor options |

Fuji showed fully on 60% of January mornings over 1991–2025 ([isfujivisible](https://isfujivisible.com/blog/mount-fuji-in-winter)). Early morning and late afternoon are best. On the 5 Jan train, sit on the Fuji side.

If you choose Kawaguchiko instead: [Oishi Park](https://en.kawaguchiko.net/park-en/oishi-park) is a flat, free lakeside view. [Chureito Pagoda](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e6917.html) is the postcard shot but 398 steps, so not for her. The [Itchiku Kubota Art Museum](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e6927.html) has kimono painted as Fuji landscapes, though its January closing days conflict between sources.

## Where to stay

An in-room bath lets her control the temperature and time. The Environment Ministry says pregnant women can bathe if they keep it short and not too hot ([official guide, PDF](https://www.env.go.jp/content/900492871.pdf)). Get her doctor's OK at 32 weeks.

| [Gora Kadan](https://guide.michelin.com/hk/zh_HK/hotels-stays/hakone) | Gora | Open-air bath in the suites | Three Michelin Keys, a former imperial retreat | Top end |

Ask for a cooked-only menu for her, because kaiseki dinners usually include sashimi.
