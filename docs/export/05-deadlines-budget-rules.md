### Rules

- **min_two_nights**: Severity: info · Two nights or more is kinder; a suggestion, never a reason to pad
- **no_peak_moves**: Severity: warn · No move over 90 minutes with the New Year crowds (out of Tokyo and Kansai 26–30 Dec, back 2–4 Jan); 31 Dec and 1 Jan are calm; moves against the flow get a booking note
- **closure_check**: Severity: warn · Flag any activity planned on a date its place is closed (closures and closed_weekdays)
- **twenty_nights**: Severity: error · Stays must cover 20 Dec to 9 Jan without gaps
- **new_years_eve_dinner**: Severity: info · Arrange New Year's Eve dinner ahead: most restaurants close (a ryokan, a booked restaurant or cooking in)
- **pregnancy_flags**: Severity: info · Show effort_for_her and avoid_for_her on every planned item
- **long_move**: Severity: warn · No day over about 4 hours door to door
- **move_density**: Severity: warn · No moves on consecutive days, and no more than three in any week
- **capacity**: Severity: warn · No more nights than a place can fill; a named day trip (Shirakawa-go from Kanazawa, the ski or Obuse day from Yudanaka) earns one more; hubs count their easy day trips
- **tokyo_ending**: Severity: warn · The night of 8 Jan near Haneda; no more than 2 nights in Tokyo at the end unless the first Tokyo stay was short
- **hospital**: Severity: warn · Every night within about 30 minutes of obstetric care; 29 Dec – 3 Jan near a perinatal centre; missing data shows as not checked
- **known_places**: Severity: info · Long stays in places Pedro and Fernanda already know should be for the first-timers
