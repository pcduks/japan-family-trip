# Output format (one block per place)

Every scout and the panel write places in this shape. Leave a line out only when it truly does not apply; write `unverified` rather than guessing.

### <Name> (<local-script name>)
- Area / nearest station · Genre · Price band per head (lunch/dinner, ¥)
- Tabelog: <score> (<n> reviews) · <URL> · Awards: <百名店 YEAR / Award Gold/Silver/Bronze YEAR / none>
- Japanese expert backing: <source + 1-line summary + link>
- Trusted-foodie trace: <who + link> or "none found"
- Reservations: <how: phone / Omakase / TableCheck / Tabelog / walk-in / queue length>; group of 6: <fits / split / whole counter>
- Pregnancy: <ok / what to avoid / what to order instead>
- Dec–Jan: <open on 24 Dec? New Year closure dates if known>
- Why it's here: 1–2 sentences in a critic's voice, without tourist clichés.

Outside Japan, replace the `Tabelog:` line with the country's primary validator from `local-validators.md`, for example `Dianping: 4.8 · 黑珍珠 1 钻 2025 · <URL>`.

For shops, markets and tours, use the same block with: what to buy or do, the price, who runs it, local reputation, and holiday hours.
