# Local validators by country

The point is to see a place the way local regulars and local professionals do. Use the primary validator for the hard number, then at least one local expert source for the reason why. Google Maps is only for addresses, hours and photos of the menu.

Platforms change, so when a scout finds that a source here has gone stale (bought, gamed or shut), it notes that in its report so this file can be updated.

## Japan (the most developed; see `docs/research/food/12-method-japanese-sources.md`)
- **Primary: Tabelog (食べログ).** The score weights reviewers by credibility. 3.50+ is excellent, 3.70+ elite, 4.0+ rare; ramen, soba, udon and yakiniku score lower, so 3.6+ there is very strong. Read the review count, 定休日 (closing day), 予約 (reservations) and 個室/貸切 (private room / buyout).
- **Awards:** Tabelog Award (Gold/Silver/Bronze) and 百名店 (Hyakumeiten, by genre and region, yearly) at `award.tabelog.com/hyakumeiten/<genre>_<region>`.
- **Experts:** Japanese-edition Michelin and Bib Gourmand; dancyu; the TRY ramen awards and ラーメンデータベース; named critics and Tabelog top reviewers. Search in Japanese.
- **Booking:** OMAKASE, TableCheck, Tabelog online reservations, Ikyu, Pocket Concierge, a hotel concierge.

## Greater China
- **Mainland China: Dianping / 大众点评** (scores out of 5 and the 必吃榜 "must-eat" list), plus the **Black Pearl Guide (黑珍珠餐厅指南, Meituan)** with 1–3 diamonds. Xiaohongshu (小红书) for current local buzz, with caution for paid posts.
- **Hong Kong: OpenRice** (smile/cry counts) and **Michelin Bib Gourmand HK/Macau**; local columnists. Weigh queue culture in cha chaan teng and siu mei as a signal.
- **Taiwan:** **Michelin Taipei/Taichung Bib Gourmand**; **iFoodie (愛食記)**; local PTT food boards (PTT Food).

## Korea
- **Blue Ribbon Survey (블루리본 서베이)**: ribbons 1–3, the most respected local guide. **Naver Map** reviews and blog posts (more local than Google). **Catch Table** for booking and its rankings. Michelin Seoul/Busan.

## Southeast Asia
- **Singapore:** Makansutra (KF Seetoh), ieatishootipost (Leslie Tay), Michelin hawker Bib; Burpple.
- **Thailand:** **Wongnai** (scores and the Wongnai Users' Choice awards), Michelin Bangkok, and the Shell Shuan Shim (เชลล์ชวนชิม) bowl sign, an old Thai seal of quality.
- **Malaysia:** Michelin KL/Penang and local blogs (e.g. KYspeaks); Penang hawker lore through local columnists.
- **Vietnam:** Foody.vn and Michelin Hanoi/HCMC/Da Nang.

## Latin America
- **Brazil:** Veja Comer & Beber (yearly), Estadão Paladar and Prazeres da Mesa awards, Guia Quatro Rodas (historical), Folha's critics; the Brazilian critics in `critics.md`.
- **Mexico:** Guía México Gastronómico (Culinaria Mexicana), Michelin Mexico (2024+); local taco-specific accounts.
- **Peru:** Summum awards, Luciano Revoredo / Ignacio Medina's columns.
- **Argentina:** Guía Óleo (local reviews, used like Tabelog), and Michelin Argentina.

## Europe
- **France:** Le Fooding, Gault&Millau, Pudlowski (Le Pudlo), and local press (Le Figaroscope, Télérama Sortir).
- **Spain:** Guía Repsol (Soles), Macarfi, and local critics; the Michelin España edition.
- **Italy:** Gambero Rosso (forks and the Tre Gamberi for trattorie), Slow Food's Osterie d'Italia (the "chiocciola"), Identità Golose, and 50 Top Pizza for pizza.
- **Portugal:** Boa Cama Boa Mesa (Expresso), Mesa Marcada, and Time Out Lisboa's critics (not the Market).
- **UK:** Harden's (survey-based, local), and critics such as Jay Rayner and Grace Dent (with caution, they lean to the trendy).

## North America
- **US:** local alt-weeklies, Eater city "Heatmaps" and essentials, The Infatuation, the NYT/Wells 100-best list (NYC), LA Times 101; Pete Wells and Kenji in `critics.md`.
- **Canada:** Canada's 100 Best, and local critics.

## Anywhere
- Look for where the local trade eats: chefs' days-off places, staff meals, market workers' breakfast.
- Queues of locals at off-peak hours are a signal; a queue of tourists with phones out is not.
- Penalise menus in four languages with photos at the door in tourist districts, unless local sources still back the place.
